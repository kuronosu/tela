# ADR-151: Right-to-left sweep of `kit`

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Superseded by ADR-155 (in part or in full; see it)

## Decision

Right-to-left sweep of `kit` (phase 10; implements ADR-090 for the recipes and follows ADR-148, which found three flaws of one kind). **The rule:** a `kit` recipe or hard-coded class uses logical utilities (`ps-* pe-* ms-* me-* start-* end-* rounded-s-* rounded-e-* border-s-* text-start text-end`) wherever its meaning is "at the start or the end of the line", and an `rtl:` variant where there is no logical one (a translation); a physical class stays only where the meaning is a side of the screen or a symmetric centering. In left to right every one is the class it replaced, so every existing golden and Chromium sheet is unchanged. **What changed (before → after):** menus and the menubar (`menu.go`, `menubar.go`): `pl-8` of inset items and labels → `ps-8`, `pr-2 pl-8` of checkbox and radio items → `pe-2 ps-8`, the indicator `left-2` → `start-2`, the shortcut `ml-auto` → `ms-auto`; `Dialog.Close` `right-4` → `end-4`, `Dialog.Header` and `AlertDialog.Header` `sm:text-left` → `sm:text-start`, `DatePicker.Root` `text-left` → `text-start`; `Switch.Thumb` adds `group-checked:rtl:-translate-x-[calc(100%-2px)]` (shadcn's right-to-left registry has the same: the thumb moved off the track); `Tabs` vertical line indicator `-right-1` → `-end-1`; `ToggleGroup` `first:rounded-l-md last:rounded-r-md` → `first:rounded-s-md last:rounded-e-md` and `border-l-0 first:border-l` → `border-s-0 first:border-s`; `Calendar` range ends `rounded-l-md`/`rounded-r-md` → `rounded-s-md`/`rounded-e-md`; `Table` header `text-left` → `text-start`, `AlignRight` `text-right` → `text-end` (its `justify-end` already was logical: `AlignLeft` and `AlignRight` are the start and the end of the line, documented) and the sticky columns `left-N` → `start-N` (the engine resolves it to `right-N` and the column sticks to the right edge); `Toaster` items: the icon `-ml-[3px] mr-1` → `-ms-[3px] me-1`, `-ml-px` → `-ms-px`, the action `ml-auto` → `ms-auto`, the close button `left-0 -translate-x-[35%]` → `start-0 -translate-x-[35%] rtl:translate-x-[35%]` (sonner moves it to the other corner under `dir=rtl`, too). **What stays physical, and why:** `Toaster` `Position` (`ToastBottomRight` is the window's right, as sonner's `bottom-right`, documented), the `top/right/bottom/left` sides of `Popover`, `Tooltip` and `Select` and their `origin-*`, `starting:translate-x-*` and arrow margins (a side of the anchor, as Floating UI's), centering (`left-1/2 -translate-x-1/2` of the radio dot, the centered toasts), a `Slider`'s and a color slider's own anchoring (below). `Align: "start"` of a floating panel follows the direction by itself (`aria.UseFloating`, tested). **Direction known to the component:** a component cannot read its direction while it builds, and the ones whose arrow keys or drag follow it need it, so `kit.DirectionProvider{Direction, Children}` (Radix's `DirectionProvider`: "ltr" or "rtl" in any case, so `i18n.UseFormatter().Dir().String()` works, dev panic on another value) sets `h.Dir` on a `contents` element and provides the direction by context, and `Slider`, `Tabs`, `ToggleGroup`, `RadioGroup`, `Menubar`, `ColorPicker` and `ColorInput` get a `Direction` field (Radix's `dir` prop; it wins over the provider and also sets `h.Dir` on the component's root; `Calendar` and `RangeCalendar` already had one and now fall back to the provider when they have no `Locale`); they pass it to their `aria` hook (`Direction`), which swaps the arrow keys and mirrors the drag. A bare `h.Dir("rtl")` mirrors the layout and every recipe, but not those keys and drags (documented in `doc.go`). A `Slider` and a color picker choose physical classes in Go from the direction they were given (`right-0` and a negative translation for a right-to-left horizontal slider; a `270deg` hue and alpha gradient and `bg-linear-to-l` for the saturation), never `end-0` or `rtl:`, so what they draw cannot disagree with what their keys and drag do; the hex field and the `ColorInput` code are `dir=ltr` (a code beginning with a letter showed its `#` at the end). **Tests:** `TestRTLGoldens` (a golden per sheet, `testdata/<name>_rtl_light_x1.png`: the light panel at scale 1 under `dir=rtl`, each cell inside a `DirectionProvider`; 31 of them, looked at), `TestChromiumRTL` (25 sheets against Chromium with `dir="rtl"`: shadcn's markup at the pinned commit has physical classes, so the harness turns them into their logical equivalents, `rtlMarkup`, which is what its right-to-left registry has, plus the `dir="rtl"` Radix writes and the inset of Radix's slider and progress; all 25 within the bounds of the left-to-right ones, `alertdialog avatar badge button card checkbox contextmenu dialog dropdownmenu field input label menubar popover progress radiogroup select slider switch table tabs textarea toggle togglegroup tooltip`), and behavior tests for the thumb of a switch, slider keys, drag, fill and knob position, the arrows of toggle group, tabs, radio group, menubar, color picker and calendar, sticky table columns, the dialog's close button and header, menu check marks and shortcuts, the toast's action and corner, a menu aligned to its trigger's start, and the provider. **Examples:** `examples/kit` follows two changes (its Rounded style swaps `first:rounded-l-md` for the logical class; its test of "a page per component" skips `DirectionProvider` as it does `StyleProvider`); no golden changed

## Rationale

The i18n example's three flaws (ADR-148) were one flaw: a recipe copied from shadcn at a commit that predates its right-to-left registry. The sweep makes the rule one line and keeps it checkable in left to right (identical output), with Chromium as the judge in right to left. Radix's answer to "the component must know its direction" (a provider and a `dir` prop) is taken as it is; reading the computed direction in a hook would be the better one (an engine feature: `tela.UseDirection()`, so a bare `h.Dir` would be enough) and is left to the owner
