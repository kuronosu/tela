# ADR-141: `kit` menus

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

`kit` menus: `DropdownMenu[K]`, `Menubar[K]`, `ContextMenu[K]` (phase 10, T6, kit half; builds on ADR-129, 130, 133 and 137; every public shape is listed for the owner's review). **One item model for the three.** `kit.MenuItem[K]` is one struct for every entry of a menu, with `Kind MenuKind`: `MenuPlain` (the zero value: `Key`, `Label`, `Icon`, `Shortcut`, `Variant` (Default or `Destructive`), `Disabled`, `Inset`, `KeepOpen`, `OnSelect func()`, `Class`), `MenuCheckbox` (`Checked *bool`/`DefaultChecked`/`OnCheckedChange func(bool)`), `MenuRadioGroup` (`Key` names the group, `Items` are the radio items whose `Key` is their value, `Value *K`/`DefaultValue`/`OnValueChange func(K)`), `MenuLabel`, `MenuSeparator` and `MenuGroup` (`Items`, `Label` as its accessible name). Fields a kind does not use are ignored (one struct and a `Kind` rather than seven types: a menu is `[]kit.MenuItem[K]{…}` in one literal, which is how shadcn's JSX reads once flattened, and generic constructors such as `MenuSeparator[string]()` would not infer `K`). Every item that can be chosen needs a `Key` distinct within its menu (a duplicate panics in dev mode, naming it); the aria menu is keyed by them, so typeahead, the focus and `aria-activedescendant` work as for any `UseMenu`. **Components.** `DropdownMenu[K]{Trigger Trigger, Items, OnSelect func(K), Placement, Align, Offset (zero is 4, `NoOffset`), Disabled, AriaLabel, CheckIcon, RadioIcon, Open *bool, DefaultOpen, OnOpenChange, Class, Props}` is `aria.UseMenuTrigger` + a modal `UsePopover` (Radix's menu is modal) with the trigger slot of ADR-137 (`triggerOf`); `Menubar[K]{Menus []MenubarMenu[K]{Key, Label, Disabled, Items}, OnSelect func(menu, item K), AriaLabel, Open *K, DefaultOpen *K, OnOpenChange func(K, bool), Class, TriggerClass, ContentClass, Props, CheckIcon, RadioIcon}` is `aria.UseMenuBar` (menus and items share `K`; the open menu is a component keyed by the open key, as ADR-133 prescribes, with shadcn's `sideOffset` 8 and `alignOffset` -4 as an `aria.Offset` cross-axis skid); `ContextMenu[K]{Children, Items, OnSelect, Disabled, NoLongPress, AriaLabel, Open *bool, OnOpenChange, Class (the area), ContentClass, Props, CheckIcon, RadioIcon}` is `aria.UseContextMenuTrigger` with the area a `div` that holds `Children` (it takes no `Trigger`: the area is the trigger, and Radix's `span` would not be a layout box). **The panel is one component** (`menuPanel[K]`): `UsePopover` with the options its caller hands it, `UseMenu` in mode `none` (every item is a `menuitem` from aria, and the kit overrides the role and `aria-checked` of checkbox and radio items, since one `UseMenu` has one selection mode and shadcn mixes plain, checkbox and radio items in one menu), `CloseOnSelect` off and the close decided per item (`KeepOpen`; Radix closes on a checkbox or radio too), and `menuRenderer`, which draws the entries; the same renderer draws the static panels of the tests. A `useMenuStore` hook in the always-mounted component keeps the uncontrolled checkbox and radio state (the panel exists only while open, so its own state would start afresh each time) and asks for a rebuild when it changes. The `Size` middleware limits the panel's height to the room the window has (shadcn's `max-h-(--radix-…-available-height)`), so a long menu scrolls and the keyboard scrolls the focused item into view. A shortcut is drawn and hidden from assistive technology (`aria-hidden`: it is not part of the item's name; there is no `aria-keyshortcuts` prop in `h`); binding the keys is the app's. **Recipes.** `MenuStyle{Content, Exit, Sides, Item, ItemVariants, ItemInset, ItemIcon, ItemIconVariants, CheckboxItem, RadioItem, Indicator, CheckIcon, RadioIcon, Label, LabelInset, Separator, Shortcut}` is the recipe of `Style.DropdownMenu` and `Style.ContextMenu` (the label of the latter adds `text-foreground`) and of `Style.Menubar.Menu`; `MenubarStyle{Root, Trigger, Menu}`. A variant that `ItemVariants` lacks panics in dev mode naming the field to extend; `ItemIconVariants` falls back to the default color. **Deviations from shadcn.** No submenus (ADR-129; `DropdownMenuSub*`, `MenubarSub*`, `ContextMenuSub*` are absent: a known gap, a menu is one level); the menubar's content is `overflow-x-hidden overflow-y-auto` where shadcn's is `overflow-hidden` (the same when it fits, and it scrolls when it does not); the menubar's trigger adds `aria-disabled:pointer-events-none aria-disabled:opacity-50` (shadcn does not dim a disabled menu); the context menu opens at the pointer on its bottom-start corner (aria's rule, ADR-133) where Radix puts it on the right of the point with a 2 px offset; `border` is `border border-border` and `data-[disabled]:`, `data-[inset]:` and `data-[variant=destructive]:` are `aria-disabled:`, a Go condition and `ItemVariants`; the destructive icon's color is `ItemIconVariants`. **Conformance** (Chromium with Tailwind 4.3.3, shadcn's markup at the pinned commit for the open panels, which the tests render through `StaticDropdownMenu`, `StaticContextMenu` and `StaticMenubarMenu`, test-only exports like ADR-137's, and for the closed menu bar; every panel's and cell's box is a probe): the dropdown sheet (an account menu, checkbox and radio items in every state, icons, destructive, inset, disabled and focused items, a `Class`) agrees within **0.024 px** (x1) and 0.014 px (x2) in every edge and a mean difference per channel of at most 0.95 (x1) and 0.89 (x2) around a cell; the context menu sheet 0.014 px and at most 1.01 / 0.69; the menubar sheet (the bar, one with a single menu, and the File, Edit and View panels with `min-w-48` and `rounded-xs` items) 0.017 px (x1) and 0.009 px (x2), at most 1.29 / 0.80 around a panel and 2.30 / 1.39 around a bar (its trigger text, anti-aliased by Skia). Behavior tests (the three components): open and close by press and keyboard, the focus on the first or last item or the menu itself, disabled items skipped by the keyboard and ignored by the pointer, typeahead, Enter and Space, the item's and the menu's `OnSelect` in order, checkbox and radio state controlled and uncontrolled (kept across closing, `KeepOpen`, a radio group's `OnValueChange` only when the value changes), the destructive variant and its dev panic with the closest name, duplicate keys, placement (`Align`, `Offset`, flip, shift, the menubar's offsets and `min-w-48`), a long menu scrolling, the menubar's roving focus, wrap, switching with the arrows and by hover, Tab leaving, the open key controlled, `DefaultOpen`, a right click at the pointer (and again moving it, flipping and shifting at the window's edge), Shift+F10 and the ContextMenu key at the area's corner, the innermost area winning, a disabled area, the aria snapshots, `StyleProvider`, three runnable examples, goldens of the sheets and of each menu open (light and dark, x1 and x2) and an idle check (no frames once open). The right click is injected through a headless window in `kit/menu_test.go`, since `telatest` has no secondary-button action yet

## Rationale

The owner's menu items were "a slice of items with kinds" and "no submenus this phase" (K-decisions of ADR-129); one struct is what makes three components share one renderer and one test sheet, and `UseMenu` with `none` is the only way to give one menu plain, checkbox and radio items. The panel being its own component is ADR-133's rule for `UseMenuBar` and `UseContextMenuTrigger`, which the dropdown follows too, so that all three focus an item when they open. Radix's close-on-select for checkbox and radio items, the modal dropdown and the non-modal bar and context menu are what each hook's pattern needs. Every existing golden and Chromium sheet is unchanged
