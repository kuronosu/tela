# ADR-074: Blend modes, mask layers and focal gradients

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Blend modes, mask layers and focal gradients (phase 6; implements the `blend` of §13's `PushLayer` and amends ADR-032). **Blend modes:** every `gfx.BlendMode` composites a layer onto its backdrop per W3C Compositing and Blending Level 1 (plus-lighter: min(1, s + d) on every channel, Level 2) on sRGB-encoded premultiplied 8-bit values (ADR-007): the group's pixels are scaled by its opacity and mask, blended, and the coverage of the clip active at `PushLayer` interpolates between the backdrop and the result (CSS's order filter → mask → opacity → blend; Skia's coverage rule for modes that cannot fold it into the source). Multiply, screen, overlay, darken, lighten, hard-light, difference and exclusion are exact integer expressions rounded once; color-dodge, color-burn, soft-light and the non-separable modes (Lum 0.3/0.59/0.11) are fusion-safe float64. Every mode leaves the backdrop where the source is transparent and gives the source where the backdrop is, so a blend layer touches only its bounds and regions, bands and damage stay bit-identical; unmasked source-over layers keep their composite. **Masks:** `PushLayer.Mask` (`gfx.Mask{List, Mode}`; `Builder.PushMaskLayer(mask, opacity, blend, filters...)`, closed by `PopLayer`) is a finished display list in the layer's local space, rendered whole (not culled, not clipped by the clips around the layer) onto a transparent surface over the composited pixels and turned into alpha or luminance coverage (0.2126/0.7152/0.0722 of the premultiplied values, i.e. luminance × alpha, like Chromium) that multiplies the group after its filters. The layer's bounds are cut by the mask's; a nil or empty mask drops the block; `gfx.Equal` compares masks command by command, and `core`'s scroll shifts treat masked layers as not shift-exact. The mask belongs to a layer instead of being a clip so that overlapping content is masked as one group: CSS `mask-image`, `background-clip: text` (the backgrounds in a layer masked by the element's glyph runs) and COLR SrcIn/DestIn all need that. **Focal gradients:** `gfx.FocalGradient{Focal, FocalRadius, Center, Radius, Stops, Interp}` is the two-point conical gradient of Canvas, SVG (fx, fy, fr) and COLRv1: a point takes the largest t whose circle passes through it with r(t) ≥ 0, offsets beyond [0, 1] pad, points outside the cone are transparent; t is a quadratic's root computed without cancellation. **COLR:** `internal/text` drops `exactBlend`, the source-over recoloring of blend modes, the outline-union clip of non-solid SrcIn masks and the banded-circle emulation: blend composites are blend layers, SrcIn/DestIn with a non-solid mask are mask layers, and two-point gradients are focal gradients between circles of their family at the ends of the color line (moved to the circle of radius 0 where radii turn negative). SrcOut, DestOut, Xor and the atop modes stay approximate (Noto Color Emoji does not use them)

## Rationale

Measured against Chromium 152 (`internal/gfx/raster` `TestChromiumScenes4`, scales 1 and 2): 18 `mix-blend-mode` cells over opaque, translucent and out-of-gamut gradient backdrops (isolated groups, and one under a rounded clip) within 0.57 mean levels per channel; 6 `mask-image` cells (alpha, luminance, radial, nested, under a rounded clip and a rotation, with opacity and multiply) within 0.49; 8 canvas two-point gradients (focal, focal radius, on the edge, cone, strip, shrinking, ellipse) within 0.15; maxima 3–5 levels inside cells (16 for color-dodge, whose result changes fastest near Cs = 1) and up to 44 on anti-aliased rounded or rotated edges. `background-clip: text` built as a gradient in a layer masked by Inter and emoji glyph runs (`internal/text` `TestChromiumClipText`) differs only on glyph edges (means 0.47–3.48 per line, anti-aliasing and ADR-048's baselines at scale 2). Emoji against Chromium (`TestChromiumEmoji`): the flags 🇯🇵 🇧🇷 🇩🇪 go from 1.58/3.25/2.18 to 0.28/0.79/0.46 mean levels at 64 px and from 1.95/3.74/2.42 to 0.57/1.69/1.05 at 20 px; every emoji is now within 1.69. Cost on one core over 512×512 gradients: a source-over layer 6.5 ms, multiply 6.7 ms, difference 7.2 ms, soft-light 12.7 ms, hue 18 ms (the same content without a layer: 4.6 ms); an alpha mask 9.5 ms, a luminance mask 8.9 ms; a focal gradient fill 4.5 ms against 1.8 ms for a radial one
