# ADR-148: Example `i18n` on `kit`

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Example `i18n` on `kit` (phase 10, migration D5; implements ADR-129's K12 and is the showcase that `kit` works in right-to-left and in other locales; builds on ADR-087, ADR-090, ADR-138, ADR-142 and ADR-145). **What became kit** (default style, theme tokens, `kit.Theme()` in `var App`, `telatest.MountApp`): the language switcher a `kit.Select[string]` (each option in its own language, `AriaLabel` from the catalog; a select reads better than the three buttons since it exercises the popover under `rtl`), the header logo a `kit.Avatar`, the cards `kit.Card` (the plural of the messages is the `Title`, Remove and Add are the `Action` with `kit.Button` and Lucide icons from `examples/i18n/icons`, generated by `tela icons gen`), the order's progress a `kit.Progress` named by its step text, the summary rows separated by `kit.Separator`, the delivery date a `kit.Field` around a `kit.DatePicker` (starts empty, so the placeholder shows; Sundays unavailable; `Today` and `Min` injected for stable goldens; the summary's delivery row follows the chosen date), Back and Checkout `kit.Button`. Plurals, numbers, dates, times and lists are untouched. `Preview` stays as `Previews` (one per language, fixed with `i18n.Provider`) and `var App` gives the window, theme and root class. The labels kit defaults to English are set from the catalog through the fields kit has: `Placeholder`, `PreviousLabel`, `NextLabel`, `AriaLabel` (new keys `pickDate`, `previousMonth`, `nextMonth`, `languageLabel`, `deliveryDate`, `deliveryHint`, `summaryTitle`, `summary.pending`). **Locale and RTL findings in kit, fixed (backwards compatible, each with a test):** (1) `kit.Progress` filled from the left under `rtl`: its indicator is as wide as the track and slid by `-translate-x-[N%]`, the recipe of shadcn, which has the same flaw; the component now adds `rtl:translate-x-[N%]` (`TestProgressRTL`; every LTR golden is bit-identical). (2) `kit.Select`'s items used the physical `pr-8 pl-2` and the check `right-2`, so under `rtl` the text sat 32 px from the right edge with the check beside it on the wrong side; the recipe is now `pe-8 ps-2` and `end-2`, identical in LTR (`TestSelectItemsMirrorInRTL`). (3) Arabic's short weekday names (CLDR `NameShort`: سبت، أحد، إثنين، ثلاثاء...) are as long as words and overlap in the 32 px cells of the calendar ("خميس" and "جمعة" ran together); `NarrowWeekdays bool` on `Calendar`, `RangeCalendar`, `DatePicker` and `DateRangePicker` writes the one-letter CLDR narrow names (س ح ن ث ر خ ج), keeping the full weekday as the header's accessible name; false is the previous behavior (`TestCalendarNarrowWeekdays`); the example sets it for Arabic only. **Worked as they are:** the calendar's previous and next buttons swap sides and their chevrons turn (`rtl:rotate-180` of the recipe), the weekday columns run from the right starting at Saturday, the grid and the date button show Arabic-Indic digits, the popover opens from the right edge (`Align` start), the Select list and the DatePicker popover inherit the direction through the portal, the date cell names are the locale's full date, and a locale change with the popover open keeps it open, keeps the chosen date and rewrites every text of the calendar (`TestLocaleChangesWithThePickerOpen`). **API gaps found, not filled:** `kit.Button` has no class for its icon, so the back arrow is chosen in Go (`icons.ArrowRight` under `rtl`) instead of `rtl:rotate-180`; `DatePicker` has no `WeekdayClass` or `DayClass` pass-through (only `CalendarClass`), so a locale cannot restyle one part of the calendar; `kit.Switch`'s thumb (`translate-x-[calc(100%-2px)]`), `Slider`, the other overlays' physical `left-*`/`right-*` and the `Toaster` positions are not exercised here and may have the same physical-direction flaw as Progress and Select (the owner may want an RTL sweep of the kit recipes: any `left`, `right`, `pl`, `pr`, `ml`, `mr`, `translate-x`, `rounded-l`, `text-left` that is not decoration); `Card.Title` is a `div` (the example passes `H2` for the summary only). **Goldens:** en x1 and x2, es x1, ar x1 and x2, dark en and ar, the calendar open in each language (ar at x1 and x2, dark ar), the language list open in each language, the delivery date chosen in Arabic, and aria snapshots in en, es and ar with and without the calendar open; the other examples' goldens are unchanged

## Rationale

The i18n example is where locale-dependent kit behavior shows up, and it found one flaw in each of three components that LTR tests cannot see; each fix is invisible in LTR and has a test of its own. Choosing the language with a `Select` and not a `ToggleGroup` makes the example exercise a popover under `rtl`, which found the item-padding flaw; the date picker is the showcase the owner asked for and shows ADR-142's locale, first weekday and direction in the three languages
