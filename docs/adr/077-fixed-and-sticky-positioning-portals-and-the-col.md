# ADR-077: Fixed and sticky positioning, portals and the color scheme

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Fixed and sticky positioning, portals and the color scheme (phase 6, T8; implements §9's `fixed`/`sticky` and §13's portal layer, whose `tela.Portal` becomes `h.Portal`; amends ADR-043's containing blocks and ADR-061's chunks; the public API is the owner's decision). **Containing blocks** (Chromium): a box with a transform, a filter or a backdrop filter contains its absolute and fixed descendants (`layout.Style.ContainingBlock`, `containsPositioned` in paint.go). What counts is stable between restyles, so that layout and paint agree: the target style of the classes and props (ADR-076; a transition counts by its end value, so one back to no transform does not count while it runs, where Chromium would), plus an attached keyframe animation of the transform (`animate-spin`, like Chromium's running transform animations); a paint-phase `TranslateFn` alone does not count, nor does an identity transform (`translate-x-0`, which Chromium's `translate: 0 0` would). **Fixed**: `layout.Style.Fixed` on an absolute node; the container algorithms record its static position and skip it; after every full layout of a node, `fixedPass` looks at its children for fixed nodes and for subtrees that report escaping ones (`output.escapesFixed`), and the root or a ContainingBlock node lays them out by walking down to them, so the container algorithms only record the static position of a fixed child and skip it (block, flex and grid; ADR-073's grid also uses `absContainer`/`passesAbs`, so a transformed grid contains its absolute children); a fixed node laid out by the root adds nothing to its scrollable overflow. Painted outside every scroll space it escapes, not cut by the clips of ancestors off its containing-block chain; hit-testing, bounds, the semantics tree and scroll-into-view agree (a box fixed to the window never scrolls the root). **Sticky**: relative for layout, without inset offsets; painted in a scroll space of its own (a `scroller` with `sticky` and `up`) whose offset is the negated sticky offset, after Chromium's StickyPositionScrollingConstraints: the sticky view is the nearest scroll container's scrollport without its padding (the window for the root), the containing block is the parent's content box (the whole scrollable area when the parent is the scroll container; a VirtualList item's wrapper is transparent), shrunk by the box's margins; right, left, bottom, then top, each from the unstuck rectangle; snapped to device pixels; recomputed on every paint, paint-only frames included, in tree order. A sticky chunk always carries its translation, and the damage never shifts a sticky space as a scrolled unit (its offset is not how its pixels move): its commands are matched. A VirtualList keeps the last item seen sticky before its window mounted (items never built are not known). **Portal**: `h.Portal(children ...tela.Child) tela.Node` is a primitive whose box is not part of its parent's structure but one of the root box's last children, in mount order, styled fixed inset-0 with the largest z-index (above the root's content, later portals above earlier ones), never hit itself, inheriting the text style of its element parent; state, context and event bubbling follow the element tree; sequential focus and the semantics tree put portals after the root's content, in mount order; it takes children and a Key only. **Environment**: `tela.ColorScheme` (`System`, the zero value, `Light`, `Dark`), `App.ColorScheme`, `tela.SetColorScheme(s)` (any goroutine, routed like `Post`, kept until an app receives posts), `tela.UseColorScheme() ColorScheme` (the effective scheme, `Light` or `Dark`) and `tela.UseReducedMotion() bool` (hooks with a slot reading Values owned by the Tree, so only their readers rebuild), `telatest.ColorScheme(s)`; `dark:` follows the effective scheme; `telatest.Dark()`, `tela snapshot --dark` and `Tree.SetEnvironment` stay the system's preference

## Rationale

Owner's API decisions (`h.Portal`, the scheme names). Chromium 152 is the oracle (`testdata/chromium/positioned`): 3 scenes (a fixed header, button and badge escaping a clip, a fixed box in a transformed card and one at its static position in a scrolled page; sticky headers stuck and pushed out, a bottom footer and a sticky column in a horizontal strip; a sticky page header and sidebar) at 3 to 5 scroll states and scales 1 and 2 agree within 0.000 px on every box and 0.36 mean levels per pixel (text anti-aliasing). Incremental frames stay bit-identical to full ones in property scenes with sticky, fixed and a sticky VirtualList, at scales 1, 1.25, 1.5, 1.75 and 2 (ADR-075). A 100,000-row VirtualList with a sticky header every 20 rows scrolls with a p95 pipeline of 0.80 ms and p95 raster of 2.0 ms at 800×600 x2 (1.5 ms without headers: the stuck header is rasterized again); the fixed pass costs about 3 % of an incremental 10k layout
