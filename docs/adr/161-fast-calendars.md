# ADR-161: Fast calendars

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Fast calendars: skipped day cells, recolored text and scroll damage that scales (patch `v0.10.2`; no public API change; amends ADR-061's damage, ADR-134's cell rendering in kit and ADR-142). Measured in the `examples/kit` gallery (1280×800, headless, frame by frame, before → after). **Scrolling** was the worst case: 85–190 ms per frame on the Calendar and RangeCalendar pages (max 530 ms) against 4–14 ms for Table. Every `relative` day cell is a hoisted participant of the root stacking context, painted in a chunk of its own after the scroll container's content and its scrollbars; the scrollbar commands between the content run and the first cell broke the shifted group (only pushes and pops were allowed between its runs), so every visible cell was damaged at its old and new position (500–950 rectangles), and `mergeRects`, pairwise and restarting after each merge, is cubic in them: 95% of the frame. Three engine changes in `internal/core/damage.go`: (1) a run of the same space under equal pushes continues the group whatever is painted between (`continues`), and `unitDamage` damages what is painted between two runs of a group where it meets the shifted rectangle, at its old position moved by the shift and at its new one (`between`, bounds only), as it already does for what is painted after the group, so the shift stays exact; (2) a push or pop outside the units matches only a push or pop whose block holds the same unit (`blockUnits`): the equal clips pushed around each hoisted box's chunk paired with another box's once a box scrolled out of view, misaligning every later box; (3) more than 128 damage rectangles are first gathered into the unions of a 10×10 grid (`clusterRects`, every pixel still covered) before the pairwise merge. Scroll frames are now 1.8–4 ms at x1 and 5–8.5 ms at x2 (Table 2.2–3 and 6.6–10), raster area 1–6% of the window (was 10–16%; Table 40% → 15%, which gains too). **Text colors:** a paragraph whose styles differ only in paints (text and decoration colors: a selected date, a range end, a hover class with a text color, an inherited color) was shaped again and laid out (a range preview shaped 10 paragraphs, broke 47 lines and re-measured the page grid per move); `commitParagraph` now keeps the layout and gives it the new paints (`text.Layout.SetPaint`, style numbering as `PaintLineRecolored`; `sameTextShape` is `sameTextStyle` without paints), with no layout mark: shaping, lines and geometry do not depend on paints, so the pixels are those of a new layout. **kit:** each date of `Calendar` and `RangeCalendar` is `calendarCell`, an unexported struct component whose fields are all comparable (the result ref, month index, month, date, formatter, a state bitmask from the result's queries, the two class strings), so a calendar build (a preview move, a press, a page that builds again because a callback field makes the calendar itself never skip) builds only the cells whose state changed; the hook's `AutoFocus` for a pending date matters only when the cell mounts, which always builds. The class strings are joined once per recipe and Class fields (`calendarClasses`, a per-calendar memo keyed by the whole recipe), not 84 `joinClass` per build. Same element tree below the components, same box tree, same classes. Interactions, ms per frame before → after (x1, idle machine): range preview move 3.0 → 1.4 (builds per move 1 calendar + every cell rebuilt → 1 + 2–3 cells, ~68 skipped; shapes 10 → 0), press on a day of the Calendar page (9 calendars) ~4.4 → ~2.4 per frame (2 calendar builds, 4 cells, 330 skipped), next month 7.3 → 6.7, hover 1.4 (unchanged: no build; it is the full re-record of ADR-061 over ~1,300 boxes). **Verified:** 120 screenshots of the date, Button and Table pages (mount, hover, press, preview, range, next month, keys, scroll and back; light and dark; x1 and x2) are byte-identical before and after; the damage check (`telatest.CheckDamage`: every incremental frame equals a full rendering) passes for 40 scroll, hover and press steps on 8 gallery pages at x1, x1.25, x1.5 and x2. No golden changes. **Tests:** `TestScrollShiftsHoistedCells` (one shift, raster area under a sixth of the window, 33–40% before), `TestMergeRectsManyScattered`, `TestRecolorMatchesFreshLayout`, `TestSetPaintMatchesNewLayout`, `TestRangeCalendarPreviewBuildsOnlyChangedCells`, `TestCalendarPressBuildsOnlyChangedCells`, `TestCalendarCellsFollowTheirState`; `TestRestylePropagation` and `TestParagraphReshapeVersusRebreak` now expect a color change to shape nothing. **Not changed:** a frame that builds, restyles or lays out still records every layer (ADR-061), one chunk per hoisted box (357 layers on the Calendar page), so a hover there costs about twice the Button page's; switching to the Calendar page mounts ~1,700 elements (20–28 ms)

## Rationale

The gallery made the cost visible, but each cause is generic: any page whose scroll container holds positioned boxes (badges, cards, cells) paid the per-box damage and the cubic merge, and any text whose color changes paid a shaping and a relayout. Fixing them in the engine keeps the kit's tree and classes as they are; the cell components are the kit's half, since a calendar has to build again on every preview move and only a comparable cell can be skipped
