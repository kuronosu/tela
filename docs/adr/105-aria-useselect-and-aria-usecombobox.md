# ADR-105: `aria.UseSelect` and `aria.UseCombobox`

- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

`aria.UseSelect` and `aria.UseCombobox` (phase 8, T8b; the shapes are the owner's rules for options in, results with `Props` out, state integrated and controlled by a non-nil pointer-like value, strings for modes; the names the owner has not seen are listed for review; amends §14, ADR-096, ADR-101 and ADR-104). **Select.** `UseSelect[K](SelectOptions[K]{Keys, TextValue, DisabledKeys, Selected/DefaultSelected/OnSelectionChange (one key, a `Selection`), Open/DefaultOpen/OnOpenChange, Placeholder, Disabled, Placement, Offset, Floating, MatchWidth, Modal, DisallowTypeAhead, AriaLabel, Ref})` composes `UseMenuTrigger` (`Type: "listbox"`: the press, Enter, Space, ArrowDown/ArrowUp and their `FocusStrategy`, `aria-haspopup`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` the list), the collection engine of ADR-104 (a roving focus, `focusOnHover`, `disallowEmpty`) and `UsePopover`; the result has `TriggerProps`, `ValueProps`, `LabelProps`, `PopoverProps` (the popover's props, a focus scope with `Restore`, a Tab handler and, with `MatchWidth`, a `WidthFn` from `tela.UseSize` of the trigger), `ArrowProps`, `ListBox` (a `ListBoxResult`: `Props`, `ItemProps(k)`, `IsFocused`...), `Value` (the selected item's text or the placeholder), `Selected`, `Open`, `SetOpen`, `Toggle`, `Close`, `Pressed`, `Placement` and the refs. The caller renders the popover in a `Portal` while `Open`; the list state lives in the component that calls the hook, so it decides the focus when the list opens, not when it mounts: `collState.focusOnOpen(strategy)` runs in the build that shows the list (the selected item, else the first or last by the strategy, else the list; the key waits for its element as ADR-104's pending key and the list asks for the focus when it mounts) and `closed()` forgets it. Choosing an item (`collConfig.onChosen(key, same)`, called from the single-selection press even when the key did not change, as React Aria's `allowDuplicateSelectionEvents`) selects, closes and, through the trigger's scope, gives the focus back. On the closed trigger, typeahead (the buffer of ADR-104 factored into `collState.typeAhead`, starting at the selected key) and ArrowLeft/ArrowRight select without opening; the space of a search does not open the list (`MenuTriggerOptions.skipKey`, unexported). Tab in the list focuses the trigger and closes it, so the key moves on from the trigger as in a native select. The trigger's name is `aria-labelledby="value label"` (React Aria's), plus the trigger itself when `AriaLabel` is set; the list is named by the label. The popover is non-modal by default (`Modal` makes it React Aria's modal one). **Combobox.** `UseCombobox[K](ComboboxOptions[K]{Keys or Items+Filter, TextValue, DisabledKeys, Selected..., InputValue *string/DefaultInputValue/OnInputChange, Open..., MenuTrigger ("input", "focus", "manual"), AllowsCustomValue, AllowsEmptyCollection, Disabled, ReadOnly, ShouldFocusWrap, Placement, Offset, Floating, MatchWidth, AnchorRef, AriaLabel, Ref})` returns `InputProps` (role `combobox`, `aria-autocomplete="list"`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` while open, `aria-activedescendant`, the controlled text and its change, the keys, focus, blur and composition handlers), `ButtonProps` (a press that toggles with every item and keeps the focus on the input, `tabindex` -1, `aria-label` "Show suggestions"), `LabelProps`, `PopoverProps` (a press in it keeps the focus on the input), `ArrowProps`, `ListBox` (`ExternalFocus`: a virtual focus driven by the input through `HandleKeyDown`), `Keys` (the items to render), `InputValue`, `ShowAll`, `Selected`, `Open` and the same controls. `Keys` are items filtered by the caller; `Items` are filtered by the hook with `Filter` (`aria.Contains` by default, `aria.StartsWith`, `aria.EndsWith`: `func(text, query string) bool`, folding case and diacritics with `golang.org/x/text/unicode/norm` NFD, marks dropped and lower case, which is a collator's base sensitivity without its tables; the module is approved, the `norm` package is new to `aria`, and `collate` is not linked); both is a dev panic, and so is `Filter` with `Keys`. The state machine is a port of react-stately's `useComboBoxState` and react-aria's `useComboBox`: `sync` is its effect (run after every build and, for `Items`, at once after a change of the text: open when the text changed and the input has the focus, close when nothing matches or an item was chosen, forget the highlight and clear the selection when the text is emptied, put the selected item's text back when the selection changed and the app does not keep both the text and the selection), `commit` is Enter and Tab (the highlighted item is chosen, else `commitValue`), `commitValue` keeps a custom text or puts the selected item's back, `revert` is Escape (the list closes, the text goes back; the key reaches an enclosing dialog only when there was nothing to put back), the arrows open with the first or last item (all items: `ShowAll`) and the highlight starts at the selected item. The popover is not dismissed by a press outside (`ShouldCloseOnInteractOutside` is false): the press moves the focus, the blur commits the text and closes the list, and the press reaches its target. IME: the key handler returns while a composition is in progress (`OnCompositionStart/End` and `ChangeEvent.IsComposing`), so no key navigates, and the preedit text filters the list like any text without disturbing it (the controlled field shows the value it already has). **Engine (small, tested).** (1) `core.AriaLabelledBy` takes several references (`aria-labelledby="a b"`): the name is the text of each mounted one, joined by a space, and a reference to the element itself gives its `AriaLabel`; `h.AriaLabelledBy` keeps one reference (checked against Playwright 1.61). (2) A text field with the `combobox` role exposes its text (`combobox "Fruit": App`, as Playwright prints an `input role="combobox"`), in the semantics tree and the aria snapshot. (3) `collConfig.onChosen`, `typeAhead`, `focusOnOpen`, `closed` in `aria/collection.go`; the container's `AutoFocus` no longer depends on the one-time mount flag. Every golden is bit-identical. **Not done / known.** The focused item of a list that opens is scrolled into view by an effect after the first frame, because `AutoFocus` does not scroll (checked: also outside a popover) and a layout effect runs before the engine reads the scroll extents: the first frame shows a long list from its top; no hidden native select or `name`; no `shouldSelectOnPressUp` (a press that starts on the trigger cannot be dragged onto an item); the button's label is English; typeahead stays a case-insensitive prefix match; `AllowsEmptyCollection` shows no message itself.

## Rationale

Selects and comboboxes are one pattern with two triggers, so they reuse the menu trigger, the popover and the collection of ADR-101 and ADR-104 and add only the rules that are theirs. The oracle for the printed roles is Playwright 1.61.1 in Chromium with the checker of ADR-096: eight trees (a select with a label, an `aria-label`, a placeholder, open with a disabled option; a combobox closed, open with a highlighted item and a disabled one, and with only an `aria-label`) print byte for byte as Tela prints them (`button "Banana Favorite fruit" [expanded]: Banana`, `combobox "Fruit" [expanded]: b`, `button "Show suggestions"`, `listbox`, `option`). Telatest covers: the select opening with a click and with Enter, Space, ArrowDown and ArrowUp, the focus strategy and the selected item winning over it, typeahead and the arrows on the closed trigger, a search with a space, typeahead in the list, disabled items, Escape, a press outside, Tab, label and `AriaLabel`, the placeholder, controlled selection and open state (a refused choice), `MatchWidth`, `DefaultOpen` and a selected item deep in a long list; and the combobox filtering and opening while typing, the highlight with the input keeping the focus (`aria-activedescendant` and `aria-controls` read from the semantics tree), Enter, Tab and a click choosing, Escape (with and without a selection, and an enclosing dialog), blur with and without custom values, clearing the text clearing the selection, the three `MenuTrigger` modes, the toggle button, `StartsWith`, case and diacritics, caller-filtered `Keys`, an empty collection, controlled text, selection and open state, `ReadOnly` and `Disabled`, `MatchWidth`, the dev panics, an IME composition (keys do not navigate, the list follows the preedit, the commit lands) and an idle open combobox building nothing.
