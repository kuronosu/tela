# ADR-163: A menu scrolls only when the window has no room for it

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

A menu scrolls only when the window has no room for it (patch v0.10.2; kit only, no API change; found in the `examples/kit` gallery's ContextMenu page; amends ADR-099's `Size` use, ADR-101, ADR-133 and ADR-141). **Bug:** `kit.ContextMenu`, `DropdownMenu`, `Menubar` and `Select` limit their height with `aria.Size(MaxHeight(a.Height), SizeOptions{Padding: 10})`, while the `Flip()` that `aria.UsePopover` puts before it keeps no margin (Floating UI's default, as Radix's `collisionPadding` 0). A menu with between 0 and 10 px more room than its height on its side therefore passed the flip (it fits) and was limited to the room minus 10 px: a scrollbar and a few clipped pixels on a menu that fits, and no flip above where the room is plenty (the 10-entry "Right click" demo, 284 px tall: a right click at 506 to 516 px in a 800 px window; a three-entry row menu of 147 px: 643 to 653). The headless run shows it as `menu.States` with `StateScrollable` and a bottom edge 1 to 10 px above the window's; it does not depend on the scale (checked at 1, 1.1, 1.25, 1.5, 1.75, 2, 2.5 and 3: no sub-pixel overflow of the scroller was found, a menu that is not limited has `scroll max` 0 at all of them) and the first frame of the entrance is not involved (every frame was checked). **Rule:** the three middleware must agree on the margin, so the size limit takes none: `kit.menuFloating` is `aria.Size(func(a) { a.MaxHeight(a.Height) })` with no `SizeOptions`, the shape of shadcn's `max-h-(--radix-...-available-height)` (Floating UI's `availableHeight` at `collisionPadding` 0), and `kit.Select` uses it too instead of its own copy. A menu that fits never scrolls (it goes below, or flips above when below it does not fit, with at most the window's edge touching it); a menu that fits on neither side goes to the side with more room, is limited to that room and scrolls, and ends flush with the window's edge, as Radix's does (it used to stop 10 px short). `aria` is unchanged: a margin for flip and size together would be a `Padding` of `PopoverOptions` (React Aria's `containerPadding`, which this port applies to `Shift` alone, 12 px), a public addition for the owner to decide. **Tests (written, for the main session to run):** `kit/menuroom_test.go`: a context menu whose area is the window, opened with a right click at scales 1, 1.5 and 2 in windows with 1 to 40 px more room than its height (not scrollable, whole height, at the pointer), with 1 to 30 px less than its height below and plenty above (flips above, whole, not scrollable), at nine points of a 1280x800 window (never scrollable, inside the window) and in a window 60 px shorter than the menu (scrollable, limited to the window); `mountMenuRawScale` is `mountMenuRaw` with a display scale. `TestDropdownMenuLongMenuScrolls` allows 0.01 px of float error at the window's edge. **Goldens:** none changes (no golden has a menu or a list limited by the window: the open Select of the gallery ends at 600 of 800 px).

## Rationale

A menu is the place where a scrollbar that should not be there is most visible, and the two numbers that disagreed (flip 0, size 10) were each reasonable alone. Keeping the margin would need the same padding on the flip that `UsePopover` owns, which changes every popover's flip threshold and the public popover options; matching Radix (zero everywhere) fixes the cause where it is, in the kit's use of `Size`, with the least behavior change. The limit stays a pure function of the room the middleware computes, so there is no second place that has to know the menu's natural height
