# ADR-146: Examples `signup` and `player` on `kit`

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Examples `signup` and `player` on `kit` (phase 10, migration D3; implements ADR-129's K12 for these two). **signup** (default style, `kit.Theme()` with the brand's indigo as `primary`, `primary-foreground` and `ring`, in `var App`, which `tela snapshot` also applies to `Previews`): every field is a `kit.Field` around a `kit.Input` or `kit.Textarea` (label, description, `Error` for the e-mail once it was left and is not an address, which marks the input `aria-invalid`: red border and ring from the recipe, `[invalid]` in the aria snapshot, the message in place of the help text); the terms are a horizontal `Field` with a `kit.Checkbox` (label and description, as shadcn's docs); the show/hide password button a ghost icon `kit.Button` with a `kit.Tooltip` and `AriaPressed`; the strength meter a `kit.Progress` 0 to 4 with `IndicatorClass` by score; submit and "Sign in" `kit.Button`; the confirmation a `kit.Card` with `Role("status")`. The bio stays `h.BindValue` through `Props` (typing builds nothing, `TestBio`). Icons: `examples/signup/icons` (eye, eye-off, circle-check). Labels no longer carry "(any language)" or "(optional)" in a lighter weight: `Field`'s label is a string, so "any language" moved to the description. Text differences from the old goldens: indigo kept, shadcn's neutral surfaces, 36 px inputs with the 3 px ring. **player** (default style with the dark scheme forced by `App.ColorScheme`, tokens `bg-card`, `text-muted-foreground`, `bg-primary` for the range and the play button; the cover art and the violet accents stay palette colors): seek bar and volume `kit.Slider`; previous, play/pause, next `kit.Button` with a `kit.Tooltip`, in two sizes (`transport`, `play`) added to a copy of the style (`kit.StyleProvider`, a recipe edit; filled icons through `Button.Icon += " fill-current"`); a mute `kit.Toggle` (new: the old player had no volume or mute); the cards `kit.Card` (header with title and a count in `Action`); the queue rows ghost `kit.Button`s. Icons: `examples/player/icons` (play, pause, skip-back, skip-forward, volume-2, volume-x, audio-lines; the equalizer and the triangles drawn with divs are gone). `Preview()` stays (a minute and 24 seconds in) next to `var App`. **Kit and aria additions** (backwards compatible, tested): `kit.Slider.ValueSource *tela.Value[float64]` makes a one-knob slider follow a `tela.Value` that something else moves many times a second (the seek bar of a clock): the knob and the range read it in paint-phase closures, so a change repaints and builds nothing, a drag stores each position in it through `OnValueChange` (still no build: `TestSliderValueSource`, `TestSeekDoesNotBuild`), and a key or press builds the slider once so `aria-valuenow` follows; it needs `aria.SliderOptions.LiveValues func() []float64`, read at the start of a press or a key in place of the values of the last build (`TestSliderLiveValues`; without it a controlled slider compared the user's change with a value that was stale between builds and refused it). Seek granularity is a whole second (`Step: 1`), so a drag lands on 166 or 167 where the divs gave fractions. The player's headline changes: a tick no longer lays out (`WidthFn` gone, knob and range move by paint: 0 layouts per tick, was one), and the seek bar builds once a second on `Transport.Beat` (a few components) so assistive technologies read a current value; a drag does not move `Beat`. **API gaps found, not filled:** no `Alert` (kit has `Card`); `Field`'s label, description and error are strings, so no lighter "(optional)", no links in the terms and no counter beside the description (the bio's counter is a `Span` placed with `absolute` in a `relative` field); `Field` has no horizontal-group variant for several controls; `Toggle`, `Slider` and `Checkbox` are not `Trigger`s, so they cannot take a `Tooltip`; `kit.Button` has no per-size icon fill (a recipe edit does it for the whole style); `Props` takes one prop (a `Fragment` of props panics), so a second prop on a kit control needs a wrapper or is dropped (the e-mail field dropped `AutoComplete`, which Tela ignores); `Progress` has no segments (the strength meter is one bar). Hand-made, kept: the brand panel of signup (decoration, gradient and shapes), the cover art of player, the time labels

## Rationale

The two examples that exercise text fields and a time-driven control test the API where settings and pricing did not: `Field` and `Input` carry a real form with an invalid state and the IME-capable fields unchanged, and the seek bar showed that a controlled `Slider` could not follow a clock without building at its rate, which was the point of the example; the `ValueSource` and `LiveValues` additions keep it so with a smaller surface than the divs it replaces
