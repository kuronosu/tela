# ADR-142: `kit` Calendar, RangeCalendar, DatePicker and DateRangePicker

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

`kit` Calendar, RangeCalendar, DatePicker and DateRangePicker (phase 10, T8, the kit half; builds on ADR-129's K9, ADR-130, ADR-134, ADR-137 and ADR-140; every public shape is listed for the owner's review). **Calendar** `kit.Calendar{Value *aria.Date, DefaultValue aria.Date, OnValueChange func(aria.Date), Min, Max, IsDateUnavailable, Disabled, ReadOnly, FocusedDate *aria.Date, DefaultFocusedDate, OnFocusChange, VisibleMonths, FirstDayOfWeek *time.Weekday, FixedWeeks, HideOutsideDays, Today, Locale, Direction, AriaLabel, PreviousLabel, NextLabel, PreviousIcon, NextIcon, Class, MonthsClass, MonthClass, NavClass, CaptionClass, WeekdayClass, DayClass, DayButtonClass, Props}` is `aria.UseCalendar` drawn as shadcn's Calendar (react-day-picker's look): a caption with the month and year between ghost previous and next buttons, the weekday names, and a ghost icon button per date; **RangeCalendar** is the same with `Value *aria.DateRange`/`DefaultValue aria.DateRange`/`OnValueChange func(aria.DateRange)` and `AllowsNonContiguousRanges` on `aria.UseRangeCalendar`. Two components, not one with a `Mode`, so the value is typed and the rule of ADR-101 stays one line each (a pointer controls, a default starts, a callback hears); the zero `aria.Date` is none. `Today` is the injected clock (the zero Date is the wall clock), `Locale` and `Direction` choose the names, digits, first weekday and direction and, when given, set `h.Dir` on the root (else the layout follows the ambient direction and the hook the locale's). **DatePicker** `kit.DatePicker{Value, DefaultValue, OnValueChange, Placeholder ("Pick a date"), Format func(i18n.Formatter, aria.Date) string (default the formatter's long date), the calendar's Min, Max, IsDateUnavailable, VisibleMonths, FirstDayOfWeek, FixedWeeks, HideOutsideDays, Today, Locale, Direction, PreviousLabel, NextLabel, Disabled, Invalid, ID, AriaLabel, Icon, Align ("start"), KeepOpen, Open *bool, DefaultOpen, OnOpenChange, Class, PopoverClass, CalendarClass, Props}` is shadcn's date picker pattern, `Popover{Trigger: outline button with the calendar icon and the date}` holding a `Calendar`: the picker keeps the date (the panel exists only while open) and the open state, a chosen date closes the popover unless `KeepOpen`, the focus goes to the focused date as it opens (`autoFocus`, an unexported field of the two calendars that puts `AutoFocus` on the focused date's cell in the first build only, so a month change does not steal the focus) and returns to the button when it closes; inside a `Field` the button takes the field's id, `aria-describedby` and invalid state (`fieldCtx`), and its name is the label (or `AriaLabel`) followed by the date; **DateRangePicker** is the same with a `RangeCalendar` of two months (`VisibleMonths` defaults to 2, as in shadcn's demo), the text `Oct 20, 2026 - Nov 5, 2026` (medium dates) and `Format func(i18n.Formatter, aria.DateRange) string`, and closes when the second date completes the range. No time of day and no typed segments (K9). **Escape:** the calendar's own key handler (ADR-134) cancels an anchored range and stops the key, so the first Escape in a range picker cancels the anchor and the next closes the popover and restores the focus, with no change in `Popover`. **Recipes** (`kit.Style` gains `Calendar` and `DatePicker`): `CalendarStyle{Root, Months, Month, Nav, ButtonPrevious, ButtonNext, Chevron, MonthCaption, CaptionLabel, MonthGrid, Weekdays, Weekday, Week, Day, Today, Outside, Disabled, Hidden, RangeStart, RangeMiddle, RangeEnd, TodayWithSelected, DayButton, DayButtonWithSelected, DayButtonWithRangeStart, DayButtonWithRangeMiddle, DayButtonWithRangeEnd}` (react-day-picker's `classNames` of shadcn's `calendar.tsx`; `Day` is its `td`, a plain element that holds the date's `Button`, which carries the `gridcell` role, so the band behind a range and the accent behind today are the cell's, as in the markup) and `DatePickerStyle{Root, Range, Empty, Content, Calendar}`. **Deviations from shadcn:** (1) `HideOutsideDays` where the brief said `ShowOutsideDays`: shadcn shows them by default and a Go bool's zero value cannot default to true (ADR-137's `ShowClose` is the same case, there off); outside dates that belong to another visible month, or to a month before Min's or after Max's, are not drawn, as react-day-picker hides them. (2) The CSS variable `--cell-size` (2rem) is written out (`size-8`, `h-8`, `px-8`, `min-w-8`): the engine has no variables; resize a calendar by editing those strings of the recipe together. (3) The cell `Day` has no `aspect-square`: a flex item with an aspect ratio and a percentage width does not shrink below its basis in the engine (a row of seven `w-full aspect-square` items stays seven times too wide), and the button inside is square already (`aspect-square w-full min-w-8`); an empty cell of a hidden date holds an empty box of the button's classes for the same size (open for the owner: the engine's min-size rule for such items differs from Chromium's). (4) `group/calendar`, `group/day` and the `group-data-[focused=true]/day:` classes (named groups are not supported) become `focus-visible:relative focus-visible:z-10` on the button, and the focus ring is the Button recipe's; `[&:first-child[data-selected=true]_button]:rounded-l-md` and its last-child twin are not ported, since Chromium shows they lose to `rounded-none` on a range's middle and the other selected buttons are `rounded-md` already; the `data-[selected-single=true]:` and `data-[range-*=true]:` classes become Go conditions and restate their `hover:` colors, because they beat the ghost variant's `hover:` classes in Tailwind's stylesheet and would lose under ADR-140's order; `th` is centered by the user agent, so `Weekday` has `text-center`; `[&>span]:text-xs` is not ported. (5) Not ported: `captionLayout` dropdowns, week numbers, the multiple-dates mode, `buttonVariant` (edit `ButtonPrevious` and `ButtonNext` or the ghost variant), and shadcn's `data-day` attributes. (6) The icons are Lucide 1.61's, as the rest of the kit's; the page of react-day-picker's markup uses lucide-react 0.474's chevrons (identical), and the date picker cells of the Chromium sheet use the kit's calendar icon on both sides. **Conformance:** `kit/testdata/daypicker/` holds `capture.jsx` (shadcn's unmodified `calendar.tsx` and `button.tsx` at the pinned commit rendered with react-dom/server and react-day-picker 9.14.0 for seven scenarios: single, range, two months, limits with `startMonth` and `endMonth`, fixed weeks, no outside days, Monday first; `build.sh` runs it) and its markup, which are the Chromium cells of the sheets `calendar` (6 cells), `rangecalendar` (1) and `datepicker` (6 buttons, with the demo's classes) in light and dark at scales 1 and 2, with goldens: box edges differ by at most 0.075 px at x1 and 0.028 px at x2 (the calendars; 0.000 for the buttons; the largest is a panel height that Chromium rounds to 1/64 px), and the mean pixel difference per channel around a cell is 1.2 to 1.7 at x1 and 0.6 to 0.9 at x2 for the calendars and 0.3 to 2.6 and 0.2 to 1.6 for the buttons (text anti-aliasing; the shapes match), plus goldens of each picker open (`datepicker_open_*`, `daterangepicker_open_*`), behavior tests in telatest (selection by pointer and keys over months and years, Min and Max and unavailable dates, controlled and read-only, Home and End, two months without repeated outside dates, first weekday, fixed weeks, es, ja, ar-EG and `-u-nu-arab` with the mirrored layout, rtl keys, labels, recipes and classes, the two presses of a range with the preview and Escape, the unavailable-date window, picker open, choose, close and focus, keyboard flow, Escape and outside press, controlled value and open, `KeepOpen`, `Format`, `Placeholder`, `Field`, `Disabled`, the two-step Escape of the range picker), runnable examples and idle tests (an open picker requests no frames once its entrance ends).

## Rationale

shadcn's date picker is a button, a popover and a calendar, and ADR-134 gave the calendar its behavior once; the kit half is the look, the value rule over a date type that is comparable, and the picker's glue (the date outliving the popover, the focus in and back, the Field wiring). The single-element cell of ADR-134 is kept under react-day-picker's `td` so that the range band and today's accent are drawn as in the reference, and every deviation is a line of a recipe an app can edit.
