# ADR-135: Color hooks of package `aria`

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Color hooks of package `aria` (phase 10, T9; the hooks that the kit's color picker is built on, ADR-129; every public shape is listed for the owner's review; amends §14, ADR-101 and ADR-132). **The color type lives in `aria`**, not on `tela.Color`: `aria.HSB{H, S, B, A}` (degrees 0 to 360, percentages 0 to 100, opacity 0 to 1) and `aria.HSL`, with `ToHSB(tela.Color)`, `ToHSL`, `HSB.Color()`, `HSL.Color()`, `HSB.HSL()`, `HSL.HSB()`, `ParseHexColor(s) (tela.Color, error)` (`#rgb`, `#rgba`, `#rrggbb`, `#rrggbbaa`, any case, the `#` optional, spaces trimmed, an error that names the problem) and `FormatHexColor(c, alpha)` (lower case, 8-bit rounding, clamped). Conversions are plain float64 functions (CSS Color's HSV algorithm) that clamp to the sRGB gamut (extended-sRGB components are clamped first) and give a gray the hue 0; a change to the root package was not needed (`tela.Hex` panics, `ParseHexColor` returns the error a text field must revert on). **`UseColorState(ColorStateOptions{Value *tela.Color, DefaultValue, OnChange(tela.Color)})`** (react-stately's `useColorPickerState`) is the color that the area, the sliders and the field share, ADR-101's rule (nil `Value` is uncontrolled, a pointer controlled and a refused change leaves the color, `OnChange` also follows an uncontrolled state). It keeps the color as an `HSB`, the shown one, the last one asked for and the last build's, so that **the hue survives the gray axis and black** (React Aria keeps `Color` objects of a color space; a Go `tela.Color` has no hue at gray): a color the caller passes back that equals the one reported is recognized (the exact HSB is kept), one that equals the one shown is the same color, and any other is converted keeping the shown hue when it is a gray and the saturation when it is black. `Set(tela.Color)` (a swatch, the hex field), `SetHSB`, `Color()`/`HSB()` (the last build) and `LiveColor()`/`LiveHSB()` (they subscribe the paint-phase closure that reads them, ADR-132's rule) are its methods; the hue slider's track and the area's background read the live color, so they follow a drag without a build. The hooks must be called in the component that made the state (its bump is that component's), and `State` is required by every hook (a missing one panics, with the fix). Drag builds are ADR-132's: an uncontrolled color builds when a drag starts and ends and when the pointer rests for 120 ms. **`UseColorArea(ColorAreaOptions{State, XChannel, YChannel, Direction, Disabled, OnChangeEnd(tela.Color), FormatValue, AriaLabel, AriaLabelledBy})`** (React Aria's `useColorArea`): `XChannel`/`YChannel` are any two different of `ColorHue`, `ColorSaturation` and `ColorBrightness` (saturation along X and brightness along Y by default; the third stays); the result has `AreaProps` (`group`, its name, the ref and the pointer handlers), `ThumbProps`, `AreaRef`, `ThumbRef`, `Dragging`, `ThumbPercent() (x, y float64)` (0 to 100 from the left and from the top, so brightness 100 is y 0; a `TranslateFn` reads it with `tela.UseSize(AreaRef)`) and `ValueText()`. A press on the area places the thumb at the point and drags it (a mouse is captured, touch and pen keep their implicit capture, a cancel ends the drag), a press on the thumb drags it by the pointer's movement (the grab is kept), each channel is rounded to its step (`snapValueToStep`); keys on the thumb: the arrows by one step (a page with Shift), PageUp and PageDown change **Y** by a page and Home and End change **X** by a page, as React Aria's area does (it has no jump to the limits), and `OnChangeEnd` follows every key that changed the color and every drag. **The area is mirrored in a right-to-left `Direction`** as React Aria's is (the X axis, the thumb and the left and right arrows; Home goes toward the left edge); the Y axis never is. The pages are React Aria's channel ranges: hue 0 to 360 by 1 and a page of 15, saturation, brightness and lightness 0 to 100 by 1 and a page of 10, alpha 0 to 1 by 0.01 and a page of 0.1. **The thumb is one `slider`** named by `AriaLabel` (else "Color area" or `AriaLabelledBy`) with the X channel's `aria-valuenow`, `-min` and `-max` and an `aria-valuetext` with both channels ("Saturation 58%, Brightness 94%"; `FormatValue(channel, v)` for another language, as English names are the default and the system locale is never read, ADR-132); React Aria has two hidden range inputs with `aria-roledescription="2D slider"`, which the semantics tree cannot express (no hidden inputs, no `aria-roledescription`; an engine change was not made for it). **`UseColorSlider(ColorSliderOptions{State, Channel, Space, Orientation, Direction, Disabled, OnChangeEnd, FormatValue, AriaLabel, AriaLabelledBy})`** (React Aria's `useColorSlider`) is a `UseSlider` with the channel's range, step and page, controlled by the shared state: `Channel` is required (`ColorHue`, `ColorAlpha`, `ColorSaturation`, `ColorBrightness`, `ColorLightness`) and `Space` (`"hsb"` by default, `"hsl"`) says which model the saturation and the lightness belong to (a brightness in `"hsl"` or a lightness in `"hsb"` panics in dev mode); the HSL saturation that a lightness of 0 or 100 hides is kept by the state, so that the lightness slider can leave black or white. The result has `Props`, `TrackProps`, `ThumbProps`, `TrackRef`, `ThumbRef`, `Channel`, `Min`, `Max`, `Step`, `Dragging`, `ThumbPercent()` (as the slider's, live), `Value()`, `ValueText()` ("Hue 180°", "Alpha 80%") and **`TrackColors() []tela.Color`**, the colors at the stops of the gradient behind the thumb from the channel's minimum to its maximum with the other channels of the color as they are now (the hue: 7 stops every 60 degrees; the lightness: 3; the others 2; the alpha from transparent to the opaque color), live, which is all the kit needs to draw the track; the name is the channel's English name unless `AriaLabelledBy` names it. `SliderOptions` gains `PageSize` (0 keeps a tenth of the range) for the hue's page of 15. **`UseColorField(ColorFieldOptions{State, Alpha, Disabled, ReadOnly, AriaLabel, AriaLabelledBy, Ref})`** (React Aria's `useColorField`) is a hex text field on `h.Input`: the result has `InputProps`, `LabelProps` (a click focuses the field), `InputValue`, `Editing` and `Ref`; while the field is not being edited it shows `FormatHexColor(state.Color(), Alpha)`, so a drag elsewhere updates it; typing makes a draft that changes nothing; Enter and a blur parse it with `ParseHexColor`: a code sets the color (a code without opacity keeps the current one, and with `Alpha` a code of 4 or 8 digits sets it) and anything else, an empty text too, reverts to the color's code. Characters that can never be part of a code are cancelled in `OnBeforeInput` (so the caret and the selection stay), more than 8 digits are refused in `OnChange`; ArrowUp and ArrowDown add or subtract one from the color as a 24-bit number, PageUp and PageDown sixteen (React Aria's field also has Home and End to the limits, which Tela leaves to the caret, and the mouse wheel). **Not ported:** `ColorWheel`, `ColorSwatchPicker` (the kit's swatches call `Set`), `ColorThumb`, color names and their descriptions in `aria-valuetext` ("dark vibrant blue"), localized channel names, the color spaces other than HSB and HSL (RGB channels), and form values. **Tests** (`aria/color*_test.go`): conversions (known values, a grid of 4096 colors round-tripped through HSB and HSL to the 8 bits of a code, the hue kept at saturation 0, out-of-gamut and NaN input), hex parsing errors, the area's corners and clamping, keys, right-to-left, other channels, touch and pen, the hue kept through gray and black, controlled refusal, aria snapshots, runnable examples, and `TestColorAreaDragDoesNotBuildPerMove` and `TestColorSliderDragDoesNotBuildPerMove` (a whole drag: 2 builds and 2 layouts, the thumb under the pointer after every move). **Names the owner has not seen:** `HSB`, `HSL`, `ToHSB`, `ToHSL`, `ParseHexColor`, `FormatHexColor`, `ColorChannel` and its constants `ColorHue`, `ColorSaturation`, `ColorBrightness`, `ColorLightness`, `ColorAlpha`, `UseColorState`, `ColorState`, `ColorStateOptions`, `LiveColor`, `LiveHSB`, `UseColorArea`, `ColorAreaOptions`, `ColorAreaResult`, `AreaProps`, `AreaRef`, `UseColorSlider`, `ColorSliderOptions`, `ColorSliderResult`, `TrackColors`, `Space`, `UseColorField`, `ColorFieldOptions`, `ColorFieldResult`, `PageSize`

## Rationale

A picker has three parts that change one color, and each of them alone would lose the hue (RGB has none at gray) or rebuild per move, so the color is one state that holds an HSB and publishes a version that paint closures read: the same mechanism as ADR-132's slider, shared instead of copied, and the reason `State` is a required argument as in react-stately. A `tela.Color` stays the picker's value type (ADR-129), with the conversions in `aria` where the hooks and the kit use them and the root package stays small. The color slider reuses `UseSlider` (rounding, keys, drag, roles, touch) and only maps its value to a channel; the area is the same drag with two axes, and its rules (page keys, mirroring, rounding) are React Aria's. A hex field that commits on blur and Enter and reverts bad input is what the owner's decision K10 asks of "a hex field", and sits on `h.Input` with `OnBeforeInput` (ADR-069) with no engine change. Rendering is unchanged: every golden of the repository is bit-identical
