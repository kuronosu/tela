# ADR-155: `tela.UseDirection()`

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

`tela.UseDirection()` (phase 10; implements ADR-154's D1; supersedes ADR-151's `kit.DirectionProvider`; amends §7 and ADR-090). **API:** `tela.UseDirection() string`, `"ltr"` or `"rtl"`. A string, not a typed `Direction` after `ColorScheme`'s precedent: it has no third, app-level value (`ColorScheme` has `System`), and a string is the vocabulary of `h.Dir`, of kit's and aria's `Direction` fields and of Radix's `useDirection`, so the value passes to them as it is (`i18n.Direction`, a typed one, lives above `tela`). **Position:** the `Dir` of the nearest primitive ancestor that has one (core's `dirAt`, the walk of ADR-089's `schemeAt`), else `"ltr"` (there is no app-level direction); Portal content reads the direction where the Portal is declared, as its Element's parent is the declaring element. **`auto`:** the hook gives what the auto element's last style computation resolved (`Tree.autoDirs`). Before its first one (the element mounts in this build, and its text may be the output of the components being built) it gives `"ltr"`, HTML's direction of a text without strong characters; when the style phase resolves an auto element for the first time, or to the other direction (`styleDir`), it marks the readers below it that read the other one, and `Frame` runs a build and a style phase again right after its first style phase (`dirRebuild`), so a wrong direction is never laid out or painted; readers marked by a later style phase of the frame (after a layout effect) build in the next frame. A reader whose output flips the direction it reads builds again every frame, as a layout effect that always sets state would. **Invalidation:** the slot (`dirSlot`) keeps the direction its last build returned; a change of a primitive's `Dir` (in `update`) and an auto resolution walk the primitive's subtree, skipping subtrees with a `Dir` of their own, and mark only the readers whose kept direction differs (a `Dir("ltr")` under a left-to-right parent rebuilds nothing); with no reader in the tree (`dirHooks`) nothing walks. Dev mode names the hook `UseDirection` (`hookDirection`, appended to the kinds); under `tela dev` the slot holds no state and is part of the hook signature (ADR-113); `telatest` needs nothing of its own (there is no app-level direction to set). **kit:** `DirectionProvider`, never released, is removed with its file and tests; the components whose keys or drags follow the direction (`Slider`, `Tabs`, `ToggleGroup`, `RadioGroup`, `Menubar`, `ColorPicker`, `ColorInput`) read `tela.UseDirection()` when their `Direction` field is empty (the field still wins and sets `h.Dir` on their root); the calendars' arrow keys follow `Direction`, then `Locale`, then the hook, and only a field sets `h.Dir` on their root. The right-to-left sheets now rely on the panel's `h.Dir("rtl")` alone: the 31 goldens are bit-identical; the behavior tests use `h.Dir("rtl")` instead of the provider and add a runtime `Dir` change, a field winning over `Dir`, and the field's dev panic. One behavior change: a calendar without `Direction` or `Locale`, under an `i18n.Provider` of a right-to-left locale and no `h.Dir`, now has left-to-right arrow keys, as its layout is (before, the keys followed the locale and the layout did not)

## Rationale

ADR-151's own preference: a bare `h.Dir` is enough, as in the browser, where `dir` reaches every component through the computed direction. Building the readers again inside the frame keeps "same tree, same pixels" for `auto`, whose result is known only once the subtree is built, at the price of a second build of those readers in the frame where an auto element first resolves to right to left or flips; comparing the kept direction keeps a `Dir` change from rebuilding readers whose direction did not change
