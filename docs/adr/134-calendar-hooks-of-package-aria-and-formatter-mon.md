# ADR-134: Calendar hooks of package `aria` and `Formatter.MonthYear`

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Calendar hooks of package `aria` and `Formatter.MonthYear` (phase 10, T8; the hooks that the kit's Calendar and date pickers are built on, ADR-129; every public shape is listed for the owner's review; amends §14, ADR-087, ADR-101 and ADR-131). **`aria.Date{Year, Month, Day}`** is the day of the Gregorian calendar, with no time or zone: a comparable value (a `time.Time` is not reliably so: `==` also compares the zone pointer and the monotonic reading), so it is a map key, the state of `UseState` and a prop that skips equal builds, and its arithmetic (`AddDays`, `AddMonths` and `AddYears`, which clamp the day like `java.time` and `@internationalized/date`: January 31 plus a month is February 28 or 29; `Compare`, `Before`, `After`, `Weekday`, `String` as ISO 8601, `IsValid`, `DateOf(time.Time)`, `Time(*time.Location)`, `Today()`) has no daylight saving to trip on; the zero Date is none; `aria.DateRange{Start, End}` is a span with both ends. **`UseCalendar(CalendarOptions)` and `UseRangeCalendar(RangeCalendarOptions)`** (React Aria's `useCalendar`, `useRangeCalendar`, `useCalendarGrid` and `useCalendarCell` with react-stately's `useCalendarState` and `useRangeCalendarState`) return one `CalendarResult`. Options: `Value *Date` (`*DateRange`), `DefaultValue`, `OnValueChange` (ADR-101's rule: nil is uncontrolled, a pointer is controlled and a change the caller does not store is refused, the callback also follows an uncontrolled calendar); `FocusedDate *Date`, `DefaultFocusedDate`, `OnFocusChange` (the same rule for the date with the tab stop, which decides the month on display; it starts at the selected date, else today); `Min`, `Max`, `IsDateUnavailable func(Date) bool`, `Disabled`, `ReadOnly`, `VisibleMonths` (1 to 12), `FirstDayOfWeek *time.Weekday` (nil: `Formatter.FirstDayOfWeek`), `FixedWeeks`, `Today Date` (the injected clock: the zero Date is the wall clock; the frame clock is not wall time), `Locale` and `Direction` (empty: the component's locale and its writing direction), `AriaLabel`, `AriaLabelledBy`, `PreviousLabel` and `NextLabel` (English by default; the hook has no catalog), `Ref`, and for ranges `AllowsNonContiguousRanges`. Result: `Props` (`application`, named `AriaLabel + ", " + Title` as React Aria's), `Title` (the visible range: `MonthYear` of the first month, joined by an en dash to the last one when several are shown), `Value`/`Range`, `FocusedDate`, `Months()`, `Month(i)`, `Heading(i)`, `HeadingProps(i)`, `GridProps(i)` (`grid` named by its heading, `aria-readonly`, `aria-multiselectable` for ranges), `RowProps()`, `WeekdayNames(i18n.NameWidth)` (ordered from the first day), `ColumnHeaderProps(i)` (`columnheader` named by the full weekday), `Weeks(i) [][]Date` (the weeks the month needs, four to six, or six with `FixedWeeks`, with the neighbouring months' dates around it), `DayText(d)`, `CellProps(i, d)`, `PrevProps()`, `NextProps()` (named, `Disabled` at Min and Max), `PrevDisabled()`, `NextDisabled()` and the queries `IsSelected`, `IsInRange`, `IsRangeStart`, `IsRangeEnd`, `Anchor`, `IsToday`, `IsOutsideMonth(i, d)`, `IsUnavailable`, `IsDisabled`, `IsFocused`. **Rules** (React Aria's): arrows by a day or a week (left and right swapped by a right-to-left direction, which defaults to the locale's), PageUp and PageDown by a month and with Shift by a year, the day of the month kept or clamped, Enter and Space select (not on repeat); focus is limited to Min and Max (the focus stops at the limit and a date outside them is `Disabled`); an unavailable date can be focused and has `aria-disabled`, but a press on it only focuses it; the month on display follows the focus (`alignStart`: the window moves to the focused month when it leaves it, limited to the months of Min and Max) and the month buttons move it and the focused date by `VisibleMonths`; a range is two presses (the first anchors, the pointer over a date of the month or the focus on it previews up to it, the second completes it with the earlier date first, Escape cancels), and while it is anchored the dates beyond the nearest unavailable one on each side (searched three years) are unavailable too, unless `AllowsNonContiguousRanges`. **Deviations** (each listed for the owner): (1) a cell is **one** element, a focusable `gridcell` (the ARIA grid pattern's roving gridcell) where React Aria has a `td` with an inner `role=button` `div`, so the kit renders one `Button` per day and `CellProps` takes the month index too (`CellProps(i, d)`: with two months a neighbouring date is a cell of both grids, and a ref belongs to one element); (2) Home and End go to the first and last day of the week (the APG's keys, as the brief asked), where React Aria moves to the start and end of the visible page (as far as the port could verify); (3) the cell's name is the full localized date (`Formatter.Date(Full)`) without React Aria's localized "Today", "Selected" and "Not available" words, which need strings the hook has no catalog for (`aria-selected` and `aria-disabled` carry the state); there is no `aria-current` because `h` has no `AriaCurrent`; (4) dates of the neighbouring months are cells and pressing one moves to its month (React Aria hides them; shadcn shows them), and the focus of such a cell does not move the month until it is pressed; (5) no drag selection of ranges, no live region that announces the month, no `aria-describedby`; (6) the move of the DOM focus to a cell that is not rendered yet is the collections' (ADR-104): `AutoFocus` on the cell that mounts, plus an effect for an element that stands for another date after the build, so the caller keys the cells by `Date.String()` and each grid by its month. **`Formatter.MonthYear(t)`** is CLDR's `yMMMM` available format ("October 2026", "octubre de 2026", "2026年10月", "2026. október", "октябрь 2026 г."), vendored beside the other patterns by the generator (`availableFormats.yMMMM` of each locale file, `-fetch` keeps only that skeleton; the output stays deterministic) and run by the existing LDML interpreter, which now takes `L` (and `c`) as the stand-alone form that CLDR means, where it used the format names: `LLLL y` is "октябрь 2026", not "октября 2026", and the one pattern of the vendored data that uses `c` (`cccc d. MMMM y` of Finnish) now writes "maanantai", not "maanantaina"; no other output changes. **Cost:** the vendored data grows from 506,602 to 514,114 bytes, `dates_gen.go` from 168,131 to 169,340, and an app that imports `aria` but not `i18n` or `kit` now links `i18n` (the hello-world probe, a window with one `UseButton`: 25.25 to 25.60 MB unstripped, 20.02 to 20.26 MB stripped, of which the calendar code and `i18n` are all the growth). **Aria snapshot:** `application "Appointment, February 2026"` with the previous and next `button`s, a `heading`, a `grid "February 2026"` of `row`s of `columnheader`s (named by the weekday) and `gridcell "Tuesday, February 10, 2026" [selected]`, `[disabled]` for a date before Min or unavailable; checked byte for byte against Playwright **1.64.1** (the only one installed in the machine of this session; ADR-131's checker was 1.61.1) for a page with the same roles and attributes, as `aria/testdata/calendar_*.aria.txt`

## Rationale

The kit's Calendar, DatePicker and DateRangePicker need behavior that is right once (the keys, the month that follows the focus, the limits, the anchored range), and a date with no zone is what a calendar selects: a value type is the way to make that comparable and free of daylight saving, and `time.Time` is one conversion away. One cell element per day gives the kit one `Button` to style and the engine half the elements. Keeping React Aria's rules for unavailable dates, limits and ranges means a booking calendar behaves as the reference does with every key and pointer. A stand-alone `L` is what ICU does and the only way for `MonthYear` to say "октябрь" in Russian without a pattern of its own. Rendering is unchanged: every golden of the repository is bit-identical
