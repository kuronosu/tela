# ADR-136: `kit` choice controls

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

`kit` choice controls (phase 10, T4; builds on ADR-129, 130 and 132): `Checkbox`, `Switch`, `RadioGroup[K]`, `Toggle`, `ToggleGroup[K]`, `Slider` and `Progress`, each a file with its recipe (`Style` gains `Checkbox`, `Progress`, `RadioGroup`, `Slider`, `Switch`, `Toggle`, `ToggleGroup`) and the state rule of ADR-101. **API.** `Checkbox{Checked *bool, DefaultChecked, OnCheckedChange func(bool), Disabled, Required, Invalid, ID, AriaLabel, Icon tela.Icon, Class, IndicatorClass, IconClass, Props}` (`aria.UseCheckbox`; the check is Lucide's, declared in `choice.go`, and an `Icon` replaces it; indeterminate is not supported, aria has none), `Switch{Size (Default, Sm), Checked, DefaultChecked, OnCheckedChange, Disabled, Required, Invalid, ID, AriaLabel, Class, ThumbClass, Props}` (`aria.UseSwitch`; the knob moves with `transition-transform`), both taking the id, description and invalid state of an enclosing `Field` (`useChoiceField` in `choice.go`), so `Field{Orientation: Horizontal, Label, Children: Checkbox{}}` is the usual labelled control. `Toggle{Variant (Default, Outline), Size (Default, Sm, Lg), Pressed *bool, DefaultPressed, OnPressedChange, Disabled, Invalid, AriaLabel, Icon, Children, Class, Props}` (`aria.UseToggleButton`, Radix's names). `ToggleGroup[K]{Items []ToggleGroupItem[K]{Value, Label, Icon, Children, AriaLabel, Disabled}, Variant, Size, Spacing int (steps of 4 px; 0 joins the items, shadcn's default), Multiple, Value *K, DefaultValue *K, OnValueChange func(K), Selected, DefaultSelected aria.Selection[K], OnSelectionChange, DisallowEmptySelection, Orientation, Disabled, AriaLabel, Class, ItemClass, Props}`: the segmented control is the single-choice group with `DisallowEmptySelection`; `Value`/`DefaultValue` are the single-choice shorthand of ADR-129's rule (`OnValueChange` gets the key that was turned on) and `Selected`/`DefaultSelected`/`OnSelectionChange` serve `Multiple` and a controlled group that may be empty; giving both styles panics; the arrows wrap (`ShouldFocusWrap`, Radix's loop). `RadioGroup[K]{Items []RadioItem[K]{Value, Label, Description, Disabled}, Value *K, DefaultValue *K, OnValueChange func(K), Orientation, Disabled, ReadOnly, Required, Invalid, AriaLabel, Render func(RadioItem[K], RadioState) tela.Node, Class, OptionClass, ItemClass, LabelClass, DescriptionClass, Props}` (`aria.UseRadioGroup`; a click on the label checks the radio): **`Render`** draws an option in place of the default row and the group keeps the behavior; `RadioState{Checked, Disabled, Invalid, ID, Props tela.Prop, Indicator tela.Component}` gives the element that is the radio its `Props` (role, aria-checked, id, tab stop and handlers; style it with `aria-checked:`) and a decorative circle, which is what the settings example's theme cards need. `Slider{Value *float64, DefaultValue, OnValueChange func(float64), Values *[]float64, DefaultValues, OnValuesChange func([]float64), OnValueCommit func([]float64), Min, Max, Step, MinStepsBetweenThumbs, Orientation, Disabled, FormatValue, AriaLabel, ThumbLabels, Class, TrackClass, RangeClass, ThumbClass, Props}` (`aria.UseSlider`; the one-value fields are a shorthand the kit adds over ADR-132's slices, and `Values` wins when given): **dragging does not build**: each knob is a component that reads `ThumbPercent` and its own size in a `TranslateFn` (the knob stays inside the track as Radix keeps it: its offset is `p * (track - knob)`), and the range of a one-knob slider is a full-size bar slid by a `TranslateFn` that the track clips, so a drag costs a repaint (`TestSliderDragRepaintsOnly`: 20 moves, 0 builds and 0 layouts); a range with several knobs sets its width with a `WidthFn` (or `HeightFn`), which costs one layout per move and still no build (20 moves, 0 builds, 20 layouts). `Progress{Value, Max (100), Indeterminate, FormatValue, AriaLabel, Class, IndicatorClass, Props}` (`aria.UseProgressBar`; the bar is shadcn's, `-translate-x-[N%]` with N = 100 minus the percentage, rounded to 0.01, and `transition-all` animates it; `Indeterminate` is an addition that shows a full bar with `animate-pulse`, shadcn draws an empty one). **Recipe parts added (public API):** Checkbox `Root`, `Indicator`, `Icon`; Switch `Root`, `Sizes`, `Thumb`, `ThumbSizes`; RadioGroup `Root`, `Orientations`, `Option`, `OptionWithDescription`, `Item`, `ItemWithDescription`, `Indicator`, `Icon`, `Label`, `LabelWithDescription`, `Content`, `Description`; Toggle `Root`, `Variants`, `Sizes`, `On`, `Icon`; ToggleGroup `Root`, `Item`, `ItemWithoutSpacing`, `OutlineItemWithoutSpacing` (the item's look is the Toggle recipe's, plus the group's `Variant` and `Size`); Slider `Root`, `Orientations`, `Disabled`, `Track`, `TrackOrientations`, `Range`, `RangeOrientations`, `Thumb`; Progress `Root`, `Indicator`, `Indeterminate`. **Deviations from shadcn (all in the recipes):** `data-[state=checked]:` is `checked:` (Checkbox, Switch, its knob through `group-checked:`) and `data-[state=on]:` is `aria-pressed:` plus `checked:` in `Toggle.On`, since a toggle button is aria-pressed and the item of a single-choice group aria-checked (Radix's `data-state` has no equivalent in Tela); Tela's last-wins class order replaces CSS's variant order, so the unconditional classes come before the conditional ones (the toggle's `bg-transparent` and `border-input` are in `Root`, before `hover:` and `focus-visible:`; the toggle's `On` comes after its variant and so wins over hover, as `data-` does over `hover:`) and `Class` still overrides everything after it, including the pressed colors; the switch's `data-[size=...]:` classes and the knob's `group-data-[size=...]/switch:` are the maps `Sizes` and `ThumbSizes`; **named groups** (`group/switch`, `group/toggle-group`) and `touch-none` are not supported by Tela and left out (`group` is plain, `group-checked:` serves the switch); the Slider thumb's `disabled:pointer-events-none disabled:opacity-50` are dropped (Radix's thumb is a span, which never matches `:disabled`, and the root's `opacity-50` dims the whole slider); `gap-[--spacing(var(--gap))]` of the toggle group is `gap-N` from `Spacing`, and its `data-[spacing=default]:data-[variant=outline]:shadow-xs` never matches in shadcn's code at this commit (the spacing is the number 0) and is left out; `data-[spacing=0]:` classes are `ItemWithoutSpacing` and `OutlineItemWithoutSpacing`, which the component adds when `Spacing` is 0 and, for the second, when the variant is `Outline`; a button's content is centered by a browser in both axes and by Tela's only horizontally, so the checkbox and radio circle are `inline-flex items-center justify-center` (the check or the dot would sit at the top of a larger box otherwise); the radio item has `peer`, which shadcn's lacks, so its label dims when it is disabled; the radio group's horizontal arrangement (`grid-flow-col auto-cols-max gap-6`) and the option rows are the documentation's (`flex items-center gap-3`) and `Field`'s (`items-start`, `mt-px`, `leading-snug` for an option with a description), since shadcn's `RadioGroup` has no label or orientation of its own; the slider thumb's ring is `ring-4` on hover and focus-visible as shadcn's and is not drawn while dragging. **Not ported:** the `Field` rules that need these controls, `has-data-[state=checked]:border-primary` of `FieldLabel` (a card that wraps the control) and `mt-px` of a checkbox in a `Field` with content (add `Class: "mt-px"` to the control; the radio group does it itself), because they would change `field.go` and `control.go`, which other components share; the checkbox's indeterminate state; an RTL slider (the kit passes no `Direction`); `touch-action`. A `RadioGroup` is not a control a label names: inside a `Field` leave out the `Label` (it would warn that it names nothing) and give the group an `AriaLabel`. **Conformance** (Chromium pages with Tailwind 4.3.3 from shadcn's markup, as Radix writes its `data-state`, `aria-*` and inline `style` attributes, light and dark, scales 1 and 2; 7 sheets, 67 cells per scheme): box edges differ by at most 0.050 px at x1 (Switch, the label's field box, from 1.15 rem rounded to 1/64 px) and 0.013 px at x2 (bounds 0.06 and 0.5), and 0.000 px for Checkbox, Progress and Slider (thumb positions, range and track included); mean pixel difference per channel around a cell, x1/x2 for the worst cell of each sheet: Checkbox 1.99/5.11, Progress 0.07/0.02, RadioGroup 2.03/2.40, Slider 1.08/0.75, Switch 2.18/3.98, Toggle 1.09/0.70, ToggleGroup 0.94/0.78 (the shapes, rings, borders and shadows are under 1 level; what is left is text, as in ADR-130: the labels of Checkbox and Switch sit one device pixel off vertically at x2 because they are `leading-none` in a `Field`, so their bound is 6 and 4.5 there, and the 1-pixel difference of the Switch's dark knob at x1 is Chromium snapping a box at a quarter pixel to the device grid where Tela anti-aliases it)

## Rationale

The owner's shadcn model needed the controls that choose before the example screens (settings, dashboard) can drop their hand-made ones: the segmented control, the theme cards (`Render`) and the checkbox, switch and slider are what those screens use. The one-value `Slider` and the single-choice `ToggleGroup` shorthands keep the common case as short as the kit's other values while the slices and `Selection` stay for what needs them; recipes carry the ordering differences between CSS and Tela's last-wins rule, so an app that edits a recipe keeps working by the same rule everywhere. Every existing golden is bit-identical
