# ADR-159: Line boxes in device pixels, and an Input's inner-editor line height

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Line boxes in device pixels, and an Input's inner-editor line height (`v0.10.1`; supersedes ADR-048; amends ADR-034's "line boxes are quantized to logical px", ADR-069 and §11; corrects ADR-130's and ADR-136's explanation of the 1-pixel text offsets). **Rule (measured against Chromium 154, headless Linux):** Blink lays text out with the font scaled by the device scale factor, so at display scale k a font of s px is a font of s·k device px: its ascent, descent and line gap round to whole device pixels (`SkScalarRoundToScalar`; the Linux "borrow one unit from the ascent when the descent rounds down" never showed), `line-height: normal` is their sum, a line height is k times its CSS value in LayoutUnits (1/64 device px), and the half-leading `(line height − (ascent + descent)) / 2` is halved in LayoutUnits (truncated toward zero) and rounded down to whole device pixels (`FontHeight::AddLeading`); the baseline is the ascent plus that half-leading. **text:** `Paragraph.Scale` gives a layout its display scale (0, negative or non-finite: 1) and `Layout.SetScale`/`Scale` change and read it without shaping or breaking again (fields only added); results stay in logical px, multiples of 1/k, so at x2 a baseline may sit on a half logical pixel and at x1.5 a `line-height: normal` line of 14 px Inter is 25 device px (16.67 px) tall. Where Blink computes a unitless line height in float32 (the number as a percentage, times the font size, over 100) and truncates it to LayoutUnits, Tela rounds the exact product to the nearest LayoutUnit: on 728 pairs of Tailwind sizes and leadings at 8 scales they disagree in 3 (4/3 of a 30 or 60 px device font is 39.984 or 79.984 px in Blink, so its baseline is one device pixel higher), and on 9555 arbitrary decimals nearest disagrees in 3 where imitating Blink's float32 steps with Tela's float32 multiples would in 14. At scale 1 nothing changes but such float edge cases. **core:** paragraphs and fields are made at the tree's scale, and `Tree.SetScale` walks the elements, re-quantizes every paragraph and field (`text.Layout.SetScale`) and queues their boxes for layout (before, a new scale only repainted). **Input:** Chromium's inner editor (`TextControlInnerEditorElement::CreateInnerEditorStyle`) gives a single-line input's text `line-height: normal` when the font size is at least the line height in whole device pixels ("do not allow line-height to be smaller than our default") or when the input's `height` is a percentage or a length larger than that line height ("remove line-height if the INPUT fixed height is taller than the line-height", border and padding included whatever the box-sizing); the placeholder sits where the value does. `field.textStyle` does the same, so under `h-9 md:text-sm` an Input centers the 17 px content area of 14 px Inter in its 26 px content box (baseline at 23.5, drawn at 24, Chromium's) instead of its 20 px line (baseline 23, the 1-pixel offset at x1), and an auto-height Input with `leading-none` has 17 px of content, as in Chromium. **Measured:** `TestChromiumBaselines` (`internal/text`, Chromium's results in `testdata/chromium/baselines.json`: 13 sizes × 18 line heights × scales 1, 1.25, 1.5, 2 and 3): baselines and content areas exact, line boxes within 1/64 device px, except the 7 cases of 1170 above (logical-px quantization had 1 baseline off by a device pixel at x1 and 203, 176, 134 and 177 at 1.25, 1.5, 2 and 3); `TestChromiumInputBaselines` (`internal/core`: 22 inputs, scales 1, 1.5 and 2, ink rows of the value and heights); `kit` light and dark panels, worst cell mean per channel x1/x2: Checkbox 1.99/5.11 → 1.99/1.51, Switch 2.18/3.98 → 2.18/1.31, Input 4.60/1.74 → 1.83/1.74, so their bounds loosened for this (6, 4.5 and 5) return to the default 3.5; `examples/counter` at x2: both baselines exact (81.5 and 141.5 CSS px), the heading 1.887 → 1.050 and the button 2.235 → 1.045 levels, and its 0.5 px baseline tolerance is gone. **Known:** at x1.5 a baseline Blink places on an exact half device pixel (LayoutUnits) can be a float32 hair below it in Tela and drawn one row higher; goldens at scales other than 1 move text by one device pixel where the rules differ, and goldens of Inputs with a fixed height move at every scale.

## Rationale

ADR-130 attributed the offsets to "Chromium rounds a font's ascent and descent to whole pixels before placing the baseline and Tela does not", but Tela did round them, in logical pixels; Blink rounds in device pixels, and its Input ignores the line height an `h-*` class makes irrelevant. Every Tailwind screen has `leading-*` text and inputs, and kit's harness makes "looks like shadcn in Chromium" a test at x1 and x2: matching Chromium's rule at every scale is worth a layout that depends on the scale, which Chromium's own layout does (ADR-048 had kept one layout for all scales at the cost of these offsets)
