# ADR-143: `kit` color picker

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

`kit` color picker (phase 10, T9, kit half; builds on ADR-129 K9, ADR-130, ADR-135, ADR-136 and ADR-137; shadcn/ui has no color picker, so the look is the kit's own design in shadcn's language, not a port, and nothing is added to `THIRD_PARTY.md`). **API.** `ColorPicker{Value *tela.Color, DefaultValue, OnValueChange func(tela.Color), OnValueCommit func(tela.Color), Alpha, Swatches []tela.Color, Disabled, AreaLabel, HueLabel, AlphaLabel, HexLabel, Class, Props}` is the inline panel (16rem wide, the room inside a `Popover`'s `p-4` and `w-72`): an area for saturation and brightness, a hue slider, an alpha slider when `Alpha`, a row with a preview and the hex field, and a row of swatch buttons when `Swatches` is not empty. The value rule is ADR-101's; **the zero `DefaultValue` is opaque black** (the zero `tela.Color` is transparent, which every color the picker made from it would be); `OnValueChange` is aria's `OnChange` (every change, also while dragging, so a controlled picker builds as often as its state changes, ADR-132), and **`OnValueCommit` is called once when the user stops** (a drag ends, a key, a swatch or a committed hex code changed the color), never for a color equal to the last committed one; the names that default to English (`Color area`, `Hue`, `Alpha`, `Hex`; the swatches are named by their code) are strings the app sets for another language. `ColorInput{<the color fields of ColorPicker>, Disabled, Trigger kit.Trigger, Placement, Align ("start"), Offset, Open *bool, DefaultOpen, OnOpenChange, AriaLabel, <the four labels>, Class, PanelClass, PopoverClass, Props}` is the swatch button (an outline `Button` with a swatch and the hex text) that opens the picker in a `Popover`: **decided as a component of its own** rather than a `Trigger` field of `ColorPicker`, because the two differ in what they own (a picker that is a popover's content exists only while open; the color has to outlive it and the button has to show it) and a form field that shows a color is what apps ask for by name (`kit.Select` has the same shape); `Trigger` follows ADR-137's rule (the default is a `Button`, any other trigger is the app's and gets the popover's props, and shows nothing of the color unless the app draws it from `OnValueChange`). **Built on the hooks of ADR-135** (`UseColorState`, `UseColorArea`, `UseColorSlider` for the hue and the alpha, `UseColorField` on the kit's `Input` classes): one `useColorParts` hook in the component that owns the color (`ColorPicker`, or `ColorInput`, which calls the hooks whether its panel is open or not and gives the results to the `Popover`'s content, drawn by a `colorPanel` component with no hooks of its own), because the state asks *its* component to build when a drag starts and ends and the hooks must be in it (a first version that made the controls components of their own showed it: the field kept the color of the last build after a slider drag, and the fix is this structure); every hook is called whatever the props are (the alpha slider's too, drawn or not) so `Alpha` and `Disabled` may change. **Recipes** (`Style` gains `ColorInput` and `ColorPicker`; part names are public API): `ColorPickerStyle{Root, Disabled, Area, AreaSaturation, AreaBrightness, AreaThumb, Slider, Track, HueTrack, AlphaTrack, AlphaFill, Thumb, Checkerboard, Row, Preview, Input, Swatches, Swatch, SwatchSelected}` and `ColorInputStyle{Trigger, Swatch}`. The area is the hue at full saturation and brightness (painted live by `BgFn`) with `bg-linear-to-r from-white to-transparent` and `bg-linear-to-t from-black to-transparent` over it, as every HSV picker is drawn; the thumb is a `size-5` circle with a white border, `shadow-md` and the kit slider's `ring-ring/50` on hover and focus, filled with the color live; the sliders have `h-3` rounded tracks, the same thumb at `size-4`, and thumbs **centered on the value** (they hang 8 px over the ends of the track, unlike the kit `Slider`'s, which stays inside it, so the thumb is where the pointer pressed and above the color it stands for); the hex field is `h.Input` with `Input.Root` and the hook's props, committing on Enter and blur and reverting what is not a code (aria's rules); a swatch is a button with `aria-pressed` for the color that is current, and its `SwatchSelected` ring. **Checkerboard** (the pattern under the alpha track, the preview and the swatches): `bg-[conic-gradient(#d4d4d4_25%,#fff_0_50%,#d4d4d4_0_75%,#fff_0)] bg-[length:12px_12px]` with a dark-scheme pair (`#404040`, `#262626`), a CSS conic gradient that repeats every 12 px, with no image to draw and nothing to scale (an SVG pattern was not needed). **Dragging does not build** (`TestColorPickerDragRepaintsOnly`, 20 moves of one drag each of the area, the hue and the alpha: 0 builds, 0 layouts, 20 paints; the drag's end is 1 build; the same for a drag inside a `ColorInput`'s open panel): the area's background, the thumbs and their fills, the preview and the trigger's swatch are paint-phase functions of `LiveHSB`/`LiveColor`. Two things cannot follow a drag without a build because a gradient has no paint-phase form (`BackgroundGradient` takes a value): the **alpha track's** gradient from clear to the color, drawn from the color of the last build (it catches up when a drag of the area or the hue rests for 120 ms or ends), and the text of a `ColorInput`'s button. **Deviation from ADR-135's hint:** the hue track is the fixed rainbow (`bg-linear-[90deg,#f00,#ff0,#0f0,#0ff,#00f,#f0f,#f00]/srgb`), not `TrackColors()`, which carries the color's saturation and brightness: a gray color would make the hue slider gray and the user could not see which hue to choose, and it would be one more gradient that needs a build; `TrackColors` stays for apps that want it. **Disabled `ColorInput`:** `kit.Popover` has no disabled state and its trigger props (`aria.UseOverlayTrigger`'s `buttonProps(false)`) re-enable the button they are merged into, so a disabled `ColorInput` draws its trigger without the popover (ADR-145 found the same gap in the examples; a `Disabled` field of `Popover` would be the general fix and is open for the owner). **Conformance** (`kit/colorgradients_test.go`, a sheet whose cells are the area, the hue track and the alpha track drawn from the recipe's classes with the colors the component paints, against Chromium CSS gradients of the same colors with Tailwind 4.3.3, light and dark, scales 1 and 2; 5 cells): box edges 0.000 px, mean pixel difference per channel 0.20 to 0.22 (area), 0.12 to 0.20 (hue) and 0.21 to 0.28 (alpha, over the conic checkerboard), bound 0.5. Two findings, both about the engine: **an arbitrary `bg-[linear-gradient(...)]` interpolates in Oklab** where CSS defaults to sRGB (the mask images use sRGB, `bg-linear-*` follows Tailwind's `in oklab`), which made the rainbow differ from Chromium's by 5.5 levels and the colors between the stops wrong for a hue (an HSV hue is sRGB-linear between the 60-degree stops); `bg-linear-[...]/srgb` gives the CSS result and Chromium gets the CSS it stands for in an inline style, since Tailwind has no spelling for it; and the parser does not read `in srgb` inside a gradient (`bg-[linear-gradient(to_right_in_srgb,...)]`, which Tailwind passes through): open for the owner. **Other tests:** goldens of the picker (default, alpha with swatches, a gray color, a selected swatch, disabled) and of the `ColorInput` (four closed states, and the open window with the panel), light and dark at scales 1 and 2; behavior (area, hue and alpha by mouse, touch and pen with a cancel and a drag off the area; keys per aria; the hex field committing on Enter and blur, reverting a bad code and keeping the opacity of a short one; swatches and their `aria-pressed`; the hue kept through black and gray; controlled with a refused change and an outside change; disabled; labels; aria snapshots; popover open and close, focus into the panel and back to the trigger, Escape, an outside press, a custom `Trigger`, a controlled `Open` and `Value`; the color kept when the panel closes); idle (no frames after a drag, nor with the panel open); runnable examples. Touch and pen go through a small headless injector in `kit/colorpicker_touch_test.go` because `telatest` has no touch action. Every existing golden is bit-identical

## Rationale

K9 asks for a picker that is a few controls over one color, and ADR-135's hooks make each control a repaint-only widget; the structure that follows is that the component that owns the color owns the hooks, which is why a popover version needs its own owner, and why the picker is not `Popover` plus `ColorPicker` glued by `OnValueChange` (that would build the owner on every move). The look keeps what shadcn gives every control (tokens, radius, the `ring-ring/50` focus, `shadow-xs`, the kit `Input` for the hex field and the kit `Slider`'s thumb treatment) and adds what a color needs, the conventions of every picker people know (white and black gradients over the hue, a rainbow, a checkerboard under what is transparent). Checking the gradients against Chromium is what found that the engine interpolates arbitrary gradients in Oklab
