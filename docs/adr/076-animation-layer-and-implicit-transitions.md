# ADR-076: Animation layer and implicit transitions

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Animation layer and implicit transitions (phase 6, T4; implements §12 "Clock", "Implicit transitions" and the predefined keyframe classes of §10; refines ADR-061 for animated transform and opacity). **`internal/anim`** (below `core`, no tree knowledge): `Ticker` and `Animator` (frame clock only, never `time.Now`; `Running` and `Endless` states so that owners ask for frames and test runners know what to wait for), `Curve` (`CubicBezier` with the CSS and Tailwind v4 curves, `Steps`, `Linear`, `ParseEasing`), `Spring` (Motion's model in closed form, `SpringForDuration` = Motion's `findSpring`, rest detection; ported, MIT), typed interpolation (`LerpColor`, `LerpShadows`, `LerpTransform`, `LerpFilters`, lengths) with `Prop`, `Equal`, `CanInterpolate`, `Blend` over `ComputedStyle` groups (the bits of `style.TransitionProps`), `Transition` with CSS's reversing rule, `Spec` (the `animation` shorthand and the Web Animations timing model), `Keyframes` and the built-in spin, ping, pulse and bounce (Tailwind v4's keyframes). Everything is fusion-safe (ADR-041, `fmath`). **Style model:** `Compute`'s result is the immutable target; while a box animates `Box.cs` is a copy of it with the animated groups replaced by their value at the frame time (`Box.anim`: running transitions, keyframe run), so layout, paint and hit testing need no changes; `Tree.Animate(now)` (phase 2, the Host passes the FrameEvent time) advances the ticker and each frame installs a new copy. **Transitions** follow CSS Transitions Level 1 (start from the current value; a running one whose target is unchanged continues; a new target replaces it, or reverses it with the shortening factor when it is the start value; no matching `transition-property` or no combined duration cancels; no transition at mount). A tree that is not driven by `Animate` (`Render`, direct `Frame`) has no transitions and shows keyframes at 0. Reduced motion removes transitions (running ones end); `motion-reduce:animate-none` is the way out of keyframes, as Tailwind does. Inherited groups (color, font size) restyle children, which follow the parent's current value and start no transition for them (nor for border, outline and decoration colors that follow the text color). **Invalidation by property class:** opacity and transform (layer) only `markPaint` the box, which is a deferred stacking context for as long as it animates (a no-op paint-phase deferred prop plus `stacking`), so a frame costs no build, restyle, layout or placement and ADR-061's damage rasterizes the old and new areas; colors and radius (paint) record the layers again; shadows and filters (ink) also place again; sizes, spacing, insets, border widths and inherited groups restyle the box each frame. `paintPhase` now places the marked boxes in every frame that does not place everything. **Interpolation rules measured against Chromium 152:** colors premultiplied, in sRGB when both colors are legacy and in Oklab otherwise (computed colors do not remember their notation, so legacy means channels that are whole multiples of 1/255, exact for hex, `rgb()` and named colors and false for the converted Oklch palette); shadow lists paired by Tailwind's slots (inset shadow, inset ring, ring, shadow) before padding with transparent zero shadows, so a ring grows instead of morphing into the shadow; transforms per component (translate, rotate, scale, skew), keyframe transforms composed on the base by adding translations and angles and multiplying scales (CSS's order for translate, rotate and scale, exact unless a rotated or scaled base meets an animated translation). **Frames and idle:** after every frame `afterFrame` asks for another while the ticker is active, so an app is idle when the last transition ends; infinite animations ask while mounted; one inside `display: none` is dormant and restarts when shown (CSS); boxes that are visible but off screen keep animating (frames paint and rasterize nothing); `Tree.OnlyEndlessAnimations` lets `telatest` skip the wait for infinite animations (it waits for transitions and finite animations, advancing the clock). **Not here:** `steps()` and `linear()` in classes, custom `@keyframes` (Go API, T9), animating `min/max-*`, gradients, `width: auto`.

## Rationale

A browser's model, since users and LLMs write `transition` and `animate-spin` expecting it, and the oracle is Chromium: 11 cases at four times (oklch and hex colors, white to black, opacity, translate, scale and rotate, ring and shadow, width, blur, spin, ping, pulse, bounce) differ from its screenshots by a mean of 0.007 to 0.44 levels per cell and by at most 3 (blur) to 71 (spin) levels, the largest on anti-aliased edges (flat interiors within a few levels). Spin and hover frames of 100 buttons: 0.15-0.24 ms (x1) and 0.26-0.41 ms (x2) for a frame with one animate-spin element, 0.9-1.3 ms and 1.1-1.8 ms per frame while buttons with the bare `transition` are hovered in sequence (about 60 % of it raster of the shadows); incremental frames equal full ones in a property test of spinners, hover and size transitions
