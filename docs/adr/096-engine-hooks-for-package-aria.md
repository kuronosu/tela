# ADR-096: Engine hooks for package `aria`

- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Engine hooks for package `aria` (phase 8, T2; E5, E6 and E7 are the owner's decisions; amends §8, §14, ADR-056, ADR-058, ADR-061, ADR-070 and ADR-080). `aria` imports `internal/core` for them (like `i18n`); only `tela.UseSize`, `h.Inert` and the props below are public. **Anchored positioning (E6)** (`core.Floating(FloatOptions{Anchor, AnchorRect, Arrow, Place})`, for `aria.UseFloating`; T7 ports Floating UI's `computePosition` and middleware as `Place`, a pure function `FloatInput → FloatResult`): the paint phase runs a float pass after placing the boxes (or updating them in a paint-only frame) and before recording any layer. Every floating box of the box tree, in tree order but after the floating elements its anchor or its ancestors float in, gets `FloatInput`: the anchor's border box as painted (`ElementRef.Bounds`), `Anchored`, `AnchorClip` (the window cut by the overflow clips of the anchor's containing-block chain: Floating UI's `hide`), the floating element's laid-out border-box size, `Clip` (the same for the floating element: the window for one in a Portal or fixed to the window, the visible part of its scroll containers otherwise), `Viewport`, its direction (`RTL`), the arrow's laid-out size and `Scale`. `Place` runs when that input, the element's window position without its own offset, its options (a build gives a new prop) or a Value read by it or by `AnchorRect` changed; its `X, Y` (the border box's top-left corner in window coordinates, before its own transform) become the offset, snapped to whole device pixels (ADR-075), of a scroll space of the box's own, as for a sticky box (`scroller.floating`; `scroller.own()` covers both: `outer` is `up`, the chunk always carries its translation, the damage matches its commands and never shifts it; `ownShift` in `semOrigin` and `targetRect`). So paint, hit-testing, `ElementRef.Bounds`/`UseBounds`, semantics bounds, scroll-into-view and damage agree within the frame; the element is right in the first frame that shows it; scrolls, `TranslateFn`, layout animations and sticky offsets of the anchor move it in paint-only frames (no build, style or layout), a window resize in its layout frame, and nothing runs when nothing changed (autoUpdate without frames of its own, 0 % idle). A floating box is a stacking context. An anchor that goes leaves the element where it was (Floating UI); one never placed goes where the result says. **Size limits:** `HasMaxWidth`/`HasMaxHeight` (the size middleware) bound the box's max-width/max-height in its layout style (the smaller of the limit and its own wins; percentages of the window for a fixed box, of the parent's padding box otherwise), then the tree is laid out again inside the paint (layout, scroll extents and classic gutters; virtual lists measure in the next frame), placed again, and the pass runs again with the new size (Floating UI's reset): at most 2 relayouts per frame (`maxFloatRelayouts`), later changes kept until a later frame. **Publishing:** `core.PublishValue(v, x)` stores a Value at once inside a frame (`Place` publishing the final placement or the arrow's offset): paint-phase readers (an arrow's `TranslateFn`) are evaluated again before anything is recorded, builds, layout-phase readers and texts in the next frame (after the frame, `publishAfterFrame`). **`tela.UseSize(ref) *Value[Size]` (E7)**, an alias of `core.UseSize`: the border box as laid out (ResizeObserver's borderBoxSize), unaffected by scrolling and transforms, zero without a box of its own; published after layout in every frame that lays out (and after a float relayout), so paint-phase readers follow in that frame and builds in the next, one frame earlier than `UseBounds` for paint readers. **Interact outside and the overlay stack** (`core.InteractOutside(OutsideOptions{OnStart, OnEnd})`, `core.Overlay(OverlayOptions{OnEscape, OnInteractOutsideStart, OnInteractOutside, Modal})`, `core.IsTopOverlay(ref)`): inside is the element tree, so Portal content and overlays opened from inside count (React). Outside listeners run at the pointerdown of a primary press (left button, touch contact, pen tip: React Aria's rule) and at its pointerup when both targets are outside, before the target's handlers, like capture listeners on the document sharing the dispatch's state (`dispatchWith`): StopPropagation keeps the event from the target, PreventDefault on the pointerdown cancels its mouse events and focus change, on the pointerup the press's click. The overlay stack is the mounted primitives with Overlay in mount order; the top is the last that is not inert (an exiting one is closing). Only the top gets outside presses (the release goes to the one that was top at the pointerdown) and Escape, as the default action of an Escape keydown that no handler prevented and that is not a repeat (HTML's close requests; a combobox inside prevents it to close its own list); while there is a top overlay Escape does nothing else. **Modality:** the topmost live modal overlay and the live overlays above it are left alive; every other element is marked outside (`Element.outside`, recomputed by an element-tree walk after the build phase when the stack changes; new elements take their parent's): not hit (a press there hits the root, outside every overlay), not focusable (the focus fix-up blurs it), out of the Tab order and hidden in the semantics tree (React Aria's `ariaHideOutside` and `inert`); the ancestors of the alive subtrees are not hit nor focusable themselves but stay semantics containers, and events from inside bubble through them (React). **Inert:** `h.Inert(bool)` (`core.Inert`) is HTML's `inert`; `Element.inert` now comes from an exiting Presence child, the prop or an inert parent (`refreshInert`) with ADR-080's effects, and inert nodes are hidden in the semantics tree (StateHidden: left out of the YAML and of locators). Playwright 1.61 ignores `inert` (checked: it prints the subtree); Tela follows the browser's accessibility tree, a deliberate difference like ADR-080's exits. **Focus:** `core.Scope(ScopeOptions{Contain, Restore, AutoFocus})` (`FocusScope` is Scope without AutoFocus): AutoFocus focuses the first tabbable element of the subtree, else the first focusable one, after the style phase of the frame that mounts it, unless the focus is already inside (React Aria's FocusScope); `core.FocusFirst`, `FocusLast`, `FocusNext`, `FocusPrevious(scope ElementRef, FocusOptions{Tabbable, Wrap, From})` and `core.ContainsFocus(ref)` are React Aria's FocusManager over the subtree in tree order, its portals after its content; containing scopes that are inert or outside the modal no longer trap Tab; `core.FocusVisible(ref)` is React Aria's isFocusVisible (focused, window active, the focus-visible heuristic). **Pointer capture** (for `UsePress`): `core.SetPointerCapture`, `ReleasePointerCapture` and `HasPointerCapture(ref, pointerID)` are the DOM's: a pressed pointer's events go to the capturing element until its buttons are released; releasing a touch contact's implicit capture lets its enter and leave events follow it. **Handlers (E5):** several props handling one event on an element all run, in the order given (tested), and `core.MergeProps(ps...)` makes one Prop of several for the `Props` fields of the hooks (a primitive's only; groups take Key only). **Props:** `h.AriaHasPopup` (menu, listbox, tree, grid, dialog; true is menu, false none), `h.AriaControls(ref)`, `h.AriaActiveDescendant(ref)`, `h.AriaModal`, `h.AriaMultiSelectable`, `h.AriaOrientation` (horizontal, vertical, undefined), `h.AriaAutoComplete` (inline, list, both, none): ASCII case-insensitive tokens, unknown ones panic in dev mode with the closest valid one and are ignored otherwise; `semantics.Node` gains `HasPopup`, `Modal`, `MultiSelectable`, `Orientation`, `AutoComplete`, `Controls` and `ActiveDescendant` (node IDs). Playwright 1.61's default aria snapshot prints none of them (it renders `[active]` only in its "ai" mode and ignores aria-activedescendant), but aria-controls is a global attribute, so `Role("presentation")` gives way to the implicit role on an element with `AriaControls`.

## Rationale

Same-frame positioning (E6) needs the anchor as painted, which only the paint phase knows; giving the floating element a scroll space of its own reuses the sticky machinery, so every consumer of geometry agrees without new code paths and moving a popover records nothing. Measured with a 2,000-row list and an 8-item popover at 800×600, scale 2: the float pass adds 2.5 µs to a 16 µs paint-only frame; a scroll frame that moves the popover takes 3.0 ms against 0.84 ms without it, nearly all raster (its area, about 300,000 device pixels, is matched command by command, not shifted); an idle tree runs nothing. The 16 aria cases, the new "widget patterns" included, are byte-identical to Playwright 1.61.1 in Chromium. Incremental frames equal full ones in a property scene with a size-limited popover, a published arrow, an anchor moved by scrolls and a TranslateFn, hovers and open/close, at scales 1 to 2; every golden is bit-identical. Known gaps: a LayoutID handoff into a floating element that mounts in the frame starts from its previous offset; a layout animation inside a floating element whose limit changes in the frame retargets from the first placement; the offset is in the untransformed coordinates of the element's space, so a floating element inside a transformed ancestor is misplaced (use a Portal); the ancestors of a modal that is not in a Portal keep their own semantics nodes; a virtual list in a size-limited floating element measures in the next frame
