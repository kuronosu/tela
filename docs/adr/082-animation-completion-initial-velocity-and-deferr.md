# ADR-082: Animation completion, initial velocity and deferred colors

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Animation completion, initial velocity and deferred colors (phase 6, T7b; the owner's answers to ADR-080's known gaps; amends ADR-080 and ADR-053). **`tela.OnDone(fn func()) AnimationOption`** (Animate, UseSpring, UseTween): when the motion comes to rest at its target, fn is queued like `Post` (on the UI thread, before the next build, so state it sets is built in the frame that shows the target); it is never called for a motion that is interrupted (another `Animate`, a `Set` or `Update`, a new target of UseSpring or UseTween, the unmount of the Value's component); when the target applies at once (reduced motion, a tree without a frame clock, a value already at rest at its target, a NaN target) it is queued right after. For UseSpring and UseTween the OnDone of the build that set the target runs. A manual exit is now `UseTween(..., OnDone(done))`. **`tela.Velocity[T Animatable](v T) AnimationOption`**: a spring's initial velocity, per component in the Value's units per second; it replaces the velocity an interrupted motion would pass on (a drag release: `Velocity(Point{X: e.VelocityX, Y: e.VelocityY})`, `DragEvent` already has the release velocity) and makes `Animate` a spring. On tweens, with a type other than the Value's, and on `Color` (whose components are premultiplied sRGB or Oklab depending on the two colors, so they have no common unit) it panics in dev mode and is ignored otherwise. **`tela.BgFn(func() Color)` and `tela.TextColorFn(func() Color)` StyleProps** are paint-phase deferred props: they win over the classes and their transitions, and a change of a Value they read marks the box (inline elements too: ADR-053's paint observers skipped boxes outside the box tree) without building, restyling or laying out; the paint update sees a new background or text color and records the layers again without placing (one paint pass). The text color is inherited, so text is recolored when it is painted: `text.Layout.PaintRecolored` and `PaintLineRecolored` replace the paints of chosen styles and of their decorations without a color of their own, moving nothing; each style of a paragraph knows its element (the paragraph box for the default style, the inline element of each span) and takes the color of the nearest TextColorFn at or above it, unless an element on the way sets a text color of its own (`ComputedStyle.TextColorSet`, false for `text-current`): CSS inheritance, evaluated at paint time. A count of the boxes with a TextColorFn spares paragraphs the walk while there are none. Colors the style phase derived from the text color (currentColor borders, outlines, rings) and the text of `Input` and `Textarea` keep their class color

## Rationale

The owner's answers. OnDone gives manual exits and sequences a completion signal on the frame clock, so tests stay deterministic. Recoloring at paint keeps a text color animation at one repaint per frame; inheriting it in the style phase would restyle the subtree every frame. With BgFn and TextColorFn an animated `Color` Value needs no rebuilds, like the other types
