# ADR-066: Inline layout

- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Inline layout (revisits ADR-044's "inline elements contribute only their text style" and "never atomic inlines"; amends §10 and §11). Inline elements (display: inline) are CSS inline boxes of their paragraph's `text.Layout` (`text.Inline`): their inline-axis margins, borders and padding take space attached to their first and last characters (Blink's open and close tags: break opportunities come from the text only, an edge goes to the line of the content next to it, shaping breaks at edged boundaries); an inline without content gets a zero-width placeholder that keeps the break opportunities of its neighbors and stays on the line before them; after bidi reordering each inline has one fragment per run of visually adjacent content per line, the leftmost taking its left edge and the rightmost its right edge (Blink's `UpdateFragmentedBoxDataEdges`); the content area is the element's first available font's quantized ascent and descent, grown by vertical padding and borders that do not change line heights; every inline adds its strut to its lines. Fragments are painted as slices of the box laid out end to end (`box-decoration-break: slice`: gradients, shadows and radii of the whole box, cut at the missing edges), line by line (decorations, text, atomic inlines), with the ellipsis inside the inlines whose content on its line the truncation cut; they are hit (padding is clickable), give inline elements their semantics bounds, and `tela inspect --layout` reports them (`fragments`, `inlineBox`; fields only added). A block container whose content is all inline-level, atomic inlines included (in-flow `inline-block`, `inline-flex`, `inline-grid`, `Button`, `Img` with display inline or inline-block), is one paragraph whose layout children are the atomic inlines, laid out through `layout.InlineContainer`/`Atoms` (fit-content in the paragraph's width, min/max-content contributions in its intrinsic widths) and placed by `vertical-align` (the `align-*` utilities, now parsed; Img middle per Preflight) with the last line box's baseline (a flex container's first; the bottom margin edge for replaced boxes, overflow other than visible or no line boxes); flex items are unchanged; atomic inlines hidden by truncation are neither painted nor hit. Underlines are snapped to device pixels like Blink's. Percentages of inline padding and margins count as 0; opacity, transforms and filters of inline elements are not painted; no `vertical-align` on spans

## Rationale

Code chips, links with padding, badges and buttons in text are everyday Tailwind markup; Blink's model fits Chromium where CSS leaves room. Measured against Chromium 152: 22 text paragraphs (`TestChromiumInline`: edges, nesting, wrapping, empty inlines, bidi splits, RTL paragraphs, struts, truncation) within 0.055 px for every fragment and cluster edge; a 22-cell gallery (`TestChromiumInlineGallery`) with every element rect within 0.051 px at scale 1, horizontal edges within 0.02 px at scale 2 and vertical ones within 1 px (Blink rounds font metrics and line boxes in device pixels, ADR-048), mean pixel differences per cell at most 1.31 levels at scale 1 (glyph anti-aliasing)
