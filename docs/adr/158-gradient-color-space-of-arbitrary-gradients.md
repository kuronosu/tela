# ADR-158: Gradient color space of arbitrary gradients

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Gradient color space of arbitrary gradients (patch v0.10.1; found by the ColorPicker's rainbow, ADR-143, which differed from Chromium by 5.5 levels; amends §13 "Paints", ADR-023 and ADR-098). **Rule:** a gradient interpolates in **sRGB**, CSS's default, unless it says `in oklab`; only Tailwind's *named* utilities (`bg-linear-to-r`, `bg-linear-45`, `bg-radial`, `bg-conic`, `bg-conic-45`, `bg-gradient-to-*`) add `in oklab`, and they stay Oklab. Tailwind v4.3.3 emits no `in oklab` for its arbitrary forms (`bg-linear-[25deg,red,blue]` becomes `linear-gradient(var(--tw-gradient-stops,25deg,red,blue))`, and `--tw-gradient-position` is `to right` with no method, also when `from-*`/`to-*` follow), so `bg-linear-[…]`, `bg-radial-[…]`, `bg-conic-[…]`, `bg-[linear-gradient(…)]`, `radial-gradient` and `conic-gradient`, alone or as a layer of `bg-[linear-gradient(…),url(a.jpg)]`, now default to sRGB (`parseGradientList`). **Parsing:** the prelude accepts `in srgb` and `in oklab` before or after the direction, shape or position (`to right in oklab`, `in srgb to right`, `circle in oklab at top`, `from 90deg in srgb`, or alone); it works inside `bg-[…]`, the arbitrary utilities and `mask-[…]`. **Unsupported:** the other spaces of CSS Color 4 (`srgb-linear`, `display-p3`, `lab`, `lch`, `oklch`, `hsl`, `hwb`, `xyz`…) are an `UnsupportedClassError` that suggests `in srgb` or `in oklab`; a hue method (`shorter hue`…) and an unknown space are an `InvalidClassError`. They are never ignored: `gfx.ColorSpace` has only Oklab and sRGB (a polar space needs a hue method, so it would be a new paint field, deferred as in ADR-023). **Modifiers:** `/srgb` and `/oklab` keep working on the named utilities (as in Tailwind) and on the arbitrary forms (a Tela extension, Tailwind rejects a modifier there); combined with a contradicting `in <space>` in the value they are an error. **Masks:** `mask-[…]` no longer overrides the space after parsing (it forced `InterpSRGB`, which made `in oklab` impossible); the mask classes built from variables (`mask-t-from-*`, `mask-linear-*`…) stay sRGB, as before. The zero value of `Gradient.Interp` and the gradients built by `tela.LinearGradient` and the `bg-linear-*` utilities are still Oklab. **Effect on pixels:** arbitrary gradients without `in` between different colors change (they now match Chromium: the middle of `#f00`→`#00f` is (127,0,128), in Oklab (139,83,162)); hard-stop gradients, `bg-linear-to-*` and masks are bit-identical. `kit`'s `bg-linear-[…]/srgb` hue track is unchanged and could drop `/srgb`

## Rationale

CSS and Tailwind agree and Chromium is the reference of the goldens; Oklab for an arbitrary gradient was a Tela-only reading of "Oklab by default like Tailwind", which holds for the utilities that spell `in oklab` and nowhere else. An unsupported space fails loudly because silently interpolating in another space would look almost right
