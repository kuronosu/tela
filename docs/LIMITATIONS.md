## Known limitations (not blocking)

Small gaps that no phase is planned for (ADR-267). They block nothing; a phase that works in the area, or a real application that needs one, picks it up and moves it into its tasks. A limitation found while working goes here unless it is a bug of the work itself or belongs to a planned phase's subject.

- **Notifications (ADR-297):** on Windows, a click on a toast of an earlier run while the app runs again does nothing visible: that run's callbacks are gone, the event arrives with `NotificationEvent.Earlier` and `desktop` drops it, and the window is not raised (a persistent notification tag and a hook would need public API; the owner, 2026-11-17).
- **Packaging (ADR-295, ADR-296):** Tela's MSIX block map has no `b4:FileHash` (makeappx adds a whole-file SHA-256 for files of several blocks; optional and ignorable); the flatpak bundle path has not run (no `flatpak-builder` here); no external squashfs reader has checked the AppImage image (`unsquashfs` not installed); programs an AppImage app starts inherit its version lock, so a long-lived child delays the cleanup of that version; a Windows zip swap interrupted midway has no recovery; downloads of releases never applied stay in the cache; `tela update manifest` drops unknown fields when it rewrites a manifest.
- **Windows (ADR-261):** on Wayland and X11 the compositor runs the resize, so `platform.ResizeFloor` is not applied there and a card in a wide margin can be shrunk past its handles (`set_min_size` / `WM_NORMAL_HINTS` at the press); only portal content punches holes in hit regions (an `absolute z-10` sibling over a resize band still resizes)
- **Menus (ADR-260):** Windows' "always underline access keys" setting is not read; context and dropdown menus opened by a touch long press show underlines; a submenu wider than the whole window still overflows on one side (its width is not limited)
- **kit (ADR-263):** `Input` and `Alert` rebuild once after mount to apply their measured padding (one extra frame), and its constants live in Go, not in the recipe; a `Preview` below the root silently ignores its Theme and ColorScheme (no dev warning); the Chromium pages of the Tela-only pieces need the Tailwind CLI to be regenerated
- **i18n (ADR-266):** plural rules are still x/text's CLDR 32 (Icelandic and Macedonian pick other currency-name and compact forms than ICU for some numbers); compact percentages use the percent pattern (ICU uses the percent unit's); currency names and compact patterns cover the 113 generated locales only, and currencies no region uses today show their code; no rounding increments, cash digits, integer width, units, per mille or options after `scientific/` in skeletons; ICU 78.3's data differs from cldr-json 48 in a few places (fr-CH grouping, the new European English variants' currency formats, `my`'s currency-name pattern)
- **Desktop (ADR-262):** an app started hidden (a tray app) gets no `AtStart` for a Linux D-Bus–activated open; the stat poller misses a write that keeps size and modification time (stores and native watchers see it)

- **Text (ADR-269, ADR-270, ADR-273):** line-through is neither snapped nor given Chromium's thickness (the font's strikeout metrics, about a row thinner at 18px x2); the caret can stop inside a grapheme that a shaping-changing span splits (Chromium skips it); a hyphenation point inside a ligature is not used (Blink reshapes), a word across spans of different styles takes the dictionary of the style where the line overflows, and only hyphenated line ends are unkerned; Chromium hyphenates the parts of a word split by an inline boundary (a span, an absolute box) separately (`<span>interna</span><span>tionalization</span>` at 150 px: "inter-" in Chromium, "interna-" in Tela); the selection highlight does not cover a hyphen, and the caret at a hyphenated line's end sits before it; no German dictionary (about 120 KB, license to check)
- **Fonts and language (ADR-269):** the system font index counts a variable font at its default weight (go-text's index has no axes); the macOS system language is not read (CFLocale), only the environment, which a Finder launch lacks; a child window's root does not take the language of an `i18n.Provider` above its `Window`; a paragraph's installed CJK family prefers full coverage over the language's region (Japanese with a few Simplified-only characters draws wholly in a Chinese font); synthesis uses the @font-face rule for system fonts too (Blink on Linux: request > face weight + 200), and color fonts are never slanted
- **Selection (ADR-271):** a span's own `select-none` or `selection:` classes inside a selectable paragraph are ignored; images in a selection get no tint; no trailing space on a Windows double click (fields neither); no Linux PRIMARY selection, and copy is text/plain only; a Mac right click does not select the word; a selection end whose `VirtualList` row unmounts clears the selection; a static-selection drag cannot be cancelled by the app; text in a `relative` span with insets: its selection is painted under the span's background, and hit-testing ignores the vertical offset; the highlight of a line's break is painted with the paragraph's own text even when a positioned span ends the line
- **Layout (ADR-268):** a scrolling item (textarea, `overflow-auto` box) inside a `display:grid` wrapper counts by its min-content (Chromium: its minimum contribution), so one unbreakable long word in a textarea still widens a `1fr` column; a ratio grid measuring its content sizes a `minmax(min-content, …)` row unconstrained; an auto grid column of an `aspect-*` item with `min-h-0` takes the width its content height transfers (Chromium: its content width); an inline's own `dir` is not isolated; a line holding only out-of-flow anchors after a forced break makes an extra line box; positioned spans are painted after their own paragraph, not interleaved with the stacking context's other positioned boxes; `top-[%]` on a span counts as 0 even when the block's height is definite
- **Drag and drop (ADR-272):** after an autoscroll step moves content under a resting drag, the drop target and a field's drop caret update only at the next platform drag event; an Input's drag band is horizontal only (Chromium's inner editor is always inside its vertical band); a step bubbling from an inner container to an outer one ignores transforms between them; `starting:` does not apply when an ancestor's `display: none` ends (ADR-080)
- **Semantics (ADR-277):** a component that builds again gives every primitive below it a new node, so that subtree is built again (equal nodes are still shared downstream); a parent copies its children slice on every change below it (a flat 10k list); a geometry mark builds the whole moved subtree again, names included (no bounds-only refresh, so a scroll rebuilds the container's subtree); any ID or `For` change builds again every field and button with an ID or inside a Label; an element named from a target that is never built (unstyled, inside an exiting subtree) is not caught by the second pass; AT-SPI events of separate changed regions follow depth, not tree order; with `TELA_CHECK_SEMANTICS` tests pay a full build per `Semantics()` call (kit's tests 169 s); an exiting Presence child can be acted on by ID until it unmounts
- **Frame memory (phase 24):** at x2 the raster's scratch (`getBuf` for backdrop filters, rounded-rect masks and layers, about 400 MB over the session) and the backdrop cache's stores dominate the allocations; images are decoded again during a session after the asset cache releases them (about 36 MB at x1, `decodeAsset` from `paintBgLayer`); the composed frame list is allocated afresh every frame (about 28 MB at x1; pooling needs to know when raster released it); `layout.blockItems` and `initTracks` could reuse per-depth buffers as flex items do
- **Static selection (phase 24):** a frame that builds, restyles or lays out still walks the whole selected range (`checkSel`), and copy, a drag start and the Mac's non-directional Shift+click walk it once per action
- **Damage (ADR-275):** after a rest, a theme switch that swaps the backdrop's input (the dashboard's wallpaper image and canvas) stays a full frame, and switching back reuses the blur only before the deferred trim and not at x2 (two generations of glass exceed the cache's two-framebuffer budget); the frame that removes a dialog's scrim stays full (snapshots no frame used are not carried); frames whose first difference moves every frame (finish-and-sort's sinking row, the board's lane highlight at drag start) stay full; a snapshot of an element that grows more than 16 px a frame is taken again every other frame; the planning is O(commands × rectangles × snapshots) a frame, not measured on huge lists
- **Backdrop cache (ADR-200, ADR-275):** backdrop filters inside layers (opacity, blend, filter or mask groups; such a list also renders on one goroutine, since the phase barriers need filters outside layers), or over content that changes every frame, or whose own geometry changes (a palette scaling in, a dragged glass card), are not cached (ADR-275 only draws the unchanged page under them from a snapshot)
- **Images (ADR-242):** a large image is decoded again when it is shown after a rest (the dashboard's theme switch spends about 25 ms decoding the other scheme's wallpaper; the owner keeps memory first, 2026-11-12)
- **Occlusion (ADR-275):** only opaque colors, linear gradients and opaque images under clips and translations, fully painting at least 64x64 device pixels, count as covers that hide damage below them (no radial, conic or focal gradients, rotated or scaled covers, or covers inside layers)
- **Layout and place (ADR-280):** a cell change in an auto or `fr` column of a plain grid still sizes the tracks again from every cached item (about 75 ms in a 2000-row `kit.Table`, though only 24 boxes are laid out; a per-track contribution cache, or `RowHeight`); about 35% of the Calendar page mount (22.7 ms) is shaping 396 short paragraphs (a cache of shaped runs in `internal/text` would cut it); a box whose colors alone changed is still placed (a theme switch places all 1,049 of the dashboard's boxes, about 0.5 ms); a partial place that finds a sticky or floating box appearing or leaving, or a reorder above one, places the tree twice; `MarkContent` does not check paragraphs with atomic inlines or anchored boxes, nor ancestors with more than 32 children; the grid sort's scratch keeps the largest grid's capacity (about 2.4 MB for 12,000 items) for the Engine's life
- **Recording (ADR-274):** composition appends every list into the frame on every paint (a dashboard hover still spends about 390 µs in the pipeline); a box with many leaf children is one group, so a change to one child records them all (the spectrum's 48 bars); a stacking context's list holds a clip pair and a marker for each hoisted participant in another scroll space and is recorded whole when one comes or goes; under rotations and skews group boundaries can grow command bounds, and so damage, a little
- **Font memory (ADR-279):** a variable font without HVAR or GPOS lookups, or any font with kerx, still gives go-text its full glyf and gvar; the header stub still costs about 130 B a glyph in go-text (Noto COLRv1: 4.6 MiB); the CFF CJK collection (NotoSansCJK-Regular.ttc, 10 faces) costs about 198 MiB of heap in go-text's CFF parse; a COLR glyph that paints with the foreground gets a translucent text color's alpha twice (in its layers and at composite): check against Chromium
- **Linux startup (phase 24):** only `tela.Run` prefetches the portal's settings (`telatest`, `rawwin` and other direct `NewHost` callers read them synchronously); a backend that fails after the prefetch leaves its D-Bus connection open until exit; the Wayland buffer trim's doubled delay never shrinks back for a window
- **GPU backend (phase 25, ADR-281 to ADR-289):** no Metal device (macOS draws with the CPU rasterizer under `telagpu`) and no D3D11 device, the owner's choice (2026-11-16); both would be second devices under the same encoder. Frames over 16.7 ms on the dashboard at 2880x1800 x2: the UHD 630's seven full glass frames (19–25 ms, its fill rate) and its dialog, palette and typing animations (13–17 ms; outer shadows at 0.13–0.2 Gpx/s, a shadow coverage cache needs the device to render into the atlas); the RTX 3060 Ti's theme switch (restyle, about 26 ms in the pipeline) and the palette's first full frame with results (292 new atlas pictures, which could be rasterized in parallel). Costs: 384-byte instances; paths hashed on every draw and their coverage keyed by the exact fractional translation (sub-pixel scrolling rasterizes paths again); one copy region per new atlas picture (slow on ANV); `finish and sort` uploads about 177 KB of atlas pictures every frame (cause not found); two pass switches per layer or backdrop filter; a filter's last step and the composite are two passes; blurs under 6 device pixels read 23–31 texels per axis; images, layers, masks and backdrop windows larger than the device's largest texture are left out (reported). Robustness: a window that fell back stays on the CPU even after a transient error; `gpu.Open` delays the first frame by about 95 ms on the RTX 3060 Ti and 76 ms on the UHD 630 (§18); under `tela dev` the child opens the GPU on every reload. Presenting: no explicit sync (`wp_linux_drm_syncobj_v1`), feedback version 6 or batched registration; an interactive resize creates a buffer per frame; the copy into a buffer is its own waited submission; the framebuffer stays allocated under zero copy; `FramePixels` and drag images read the whole target back; the implicit modifier is not supported (readback); X11 presents are paced by a 60 Hz timer (a present is skipped or repeated now and then), XIDs are never reused, and core cannot answer a backend that asks for a full frame when nothing changed. CI runs the GPU packages without a device (no lavapipe).
- **Allocations (ADR-291):** the dashboard allocates about 1,280 objects per frame of a hover sweep and 480 per frame of scrolling (`hoverAllocsPerFrame`, `scrollAllocsPerFrame` in `examples/dashboard/budget_test.go`), mostly in paint recording (`gfx.Builder.AppendList`, `Path.Clone`, the glyph clone of `DrawGlyphRun`, `painter.paintBand`); the two budgets count with the local Go toolchain (1.27.4) and may differ on another one
- **Text fields:** `AutoFocus` puts the caret at the start of a field's existing value (the report dialog of the dashboard types before "Q3 revenue summary"); check where Chromium puts it
- **Floating:** size limits ignore the device-pixel snap of the position, so a menu limited to the room can end up to half a device pixel past the window at x1.5
